## CD-EM-000220 /espresso-machine/profitec-go/

- Official or exact seller source: https://www.profitec-espresso.com/media/pages/produkte/go/2d6c2c0c60-1736512718/ba_go.pdf
- Evidence and scope: Original GO English manual, section 6.4.2: CLn defaults to 0, programmable in steps of 10, and counts brews of at least 15 seconds.
- Editorial change: The original GO's CLn reminder is optional : Profitec's manual says its default is zero, meaning no cleaning prompt has been set, and only brews lasting at least 15 seconds enter the count. ## CD-EM-000223 /espresso-machine/profitec-pro-600/

Match the final Quick Steam unit's documentation before using a button sequence from an earlier revision. Checked October 14, 2026. [source]

## CD-EM-000224 /espresso-machine/profitec-pro-700/

- Official or exact seller source: https://www.profitec-espresso.com/media/pages/produkte/pro700/bf45e66cc1-1651146714/ba-pro700.pdf
- Evidence and scope: English Pro 700 manual important water notice: max 4°dH; no filter on rotary-pump tank adapter; in-tank pads or prefiltered water; specialist descaling for calcified machine.
- Editorial change: A used Pro 700's rotary pump changes the water-treatment check. Profitec's manual warns against attaching a filter to the tank adapter on rotary-pump machines; it allows an in-tank filter pad or suitably filtered water instead. It also directs descaling of a calcified machine to a specialist because loosened scale can obstruct internal passages. Ask the seller which water path and treatment were actually used, and document that history before deciding whether a service quote belongs in the purchase budget. Checked October 14, 2026. [source]

- Evidence and scope: English Pro 800 manual troubleshooting: stalled lever from excessive resistance; switch off, wait for neutral, change grind; extraction section forbids portafilter removal before neutral.
- Editorial change: If a Pro 800 lever stalls partway through a shot, the official manual treats excessive puck resistance, such as an overly fine grind, as a possible cause. Its troubleshooting direction is to switch off, wait for the lever to return to neutral, then correct the grind for the next shot. Do not force the spring arm back or unlock a pressurized portafilter to rescue the drink. That is a safety and grinder-fit cost of a spring lever. Checked October 14, 2026. [source]
