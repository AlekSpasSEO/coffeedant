# Coffeedant Journal — Volume 01

Published content date: 13 October 2026. Research-led editorial desk, without fabricated field reporting or hands-on brewing measurements.

## Contents

Three histories: Istanbul's first recorded coffeehouses under Süleyman; Ottoman and London coffeehouse public life; botanical movement and colonial labour.

Four Zeitgeist investigations: civet coffee welfare and authenticity; robusta's breeding and water constraints; decaffeination methods and cautious market interpretation; fermentation terminology and the January 2026 SCA report.

Three Brew Lab guides: dark-roast Americano; brewing water; freshness and storage.

## Experience

`/journal/` contains all ten full articles, with a working no-JavaScript reading fallback. Story and chapter controls change visible screens without modifying the URL. The homepage entry opens the same collection in a native modal dialog; Escape and Close return to the homepage. The collection route also appears in primary navigation and the sitemap.

Cards filter by category and text. Readers include chapter navigation, progress, facts, original visuals, comparison tables, visible FAQs, cited sources and related-story controls. The Americano guide includes an adjustable weight calculator, pausable animation, and original 16-second silent MP4 with a visible text transcript. Water controls demonstrate independent qualitative dimensions, without predicting numerical taste or equipment safety.

## Media

Ten 1536×1024 original AI-assisted cover illustrations, optimized as WebP and stored in `public/journal/`. The built-in ImageGen prompts are recorded in `journal-media.json`. Historical illustrations are labelled reconstructions. Code-native schematics contain no unlabelled synthetic measurements.

The original MP4 and poster are generated by `scripts/create-journal-video.py` with Pillow and imageio-ffmpeg. Media is prebuilt and committed; deployments need no Python runtime. On Windows, the optional recreation command is Python `scripts/create-journal-video.py`, after installing imageio-ffmpeg into `.cache/media-tools`. The film has no audio; its text transcript is present directly beneath it.
