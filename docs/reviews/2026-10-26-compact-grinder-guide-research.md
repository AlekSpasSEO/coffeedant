## Limits and decisions

- Width, depth and height are body specifications. Hopper loading, bellows use, collar adjustment, cord bend, catch cup and burr cleaning need additional free space. No manufacturer provided one comparable installed-clearance envelope.
- The Baratza Canada page provides exact-model dimensions while the US page confirms the US product identity; the guide does not claim Coffeedant measured a US body. The UK independent sample is clearly labeled context, not a US reliability rate.
- The guide omits numeric US price and discount ranking because a comparable delivered checkout total for exact variants was not verified. Existing affiliate links remain in full reviews with check-price treatment.
- Product images are reused from the registered review records with their existing attribution. The listicle has `Article`, `ItemList`, `BreadcrumbList` and matching visible `FAQPage` schema.
- The grinder hub provides the index link. Astro generates the canonical sitemap entry. No product review, finder record or offer fact changes, so the finder catalog is not represented as refreshed by this guide.
