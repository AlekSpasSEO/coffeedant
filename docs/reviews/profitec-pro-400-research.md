# Profitec Pro 400 current US research ledger

Research checked September 24, 2026. The published review is research-led; Coffeedant did not test, measure, photograph or disassemble this machine.

## Identity and official evidence

- Scored identity: current Profitec Pro 400, item CM500004-00, in the US 115 V configuration.
- Excluded identity: Profitec JUMP. Both models share the May 2026 manual and heat-exchanger platform, but JUMP adds a display, shot counter and different interface.
- Current manufacturer delivery includes one modular bottomless portafilter with a silicone spout attachment, 7 g, 14 g and 21 g baskets, blind filter, tamper and brush.
- Older retailer prose and photographs can still show separate single- and double-spout handles. Buyers must verify the delivered accessory revision.

## Specification and workflow record

- 1.6 L insulated stainless heat-exchanger boiler, stainless heating element, Profitec E61-format group with stainless mushroom, vibration pump, adjustable expansion valve and dual gauge.
- Three PID-controlled boiler presets: 120, 124 or 128°C. The May 2026 manual associates them with about 90, 94 or 98°C brew water after its controlled two-second reference flush, while warning that conditions and flush duration change the result.
- Switchable fixed preinfusion: five seconds of pump wetting, three-second pause, then extraction. It is disabled by default.
- Manufacturer heat-up guidance is about 25 minutes depending on ambient temperature. Pro 400 has no built-in shot timer.
- 2.8 L reservoir, 1,500 W, 115 V and 60 Hz for the US record. Dimensions are 228 × 448 × 372 mm without portafilter and 228 × 558 × 372 mm with it. Weight is about 21.5 kg.

## Dated US availability

- Whole Latte Love: stainless, no-flow-control base configuration displayed at $1,599 with three-year parts-and-labor coverage; configuration and stock state vary.
- Clive Coffee: $1,699 displayed for the Pro 400.
- Finish, accent and retailer-installed flow-control selections can increase the configured price.

All price and offer states above were checked September 24, 2026. They can change and do not establish a normal long-term market price.

## Independent and community evidence

- LifeStyle Lab published a hands-on review on September 18, 2022 and uploaded the unique exact-model video on September 4, 2022. Its affiliate participation is disclosed.
- Whole Latte Love published a staff hands-on review. It supplies attributed workflow observation from a retailer, not independent reliability evidence.
- Owner discussions cover fixed preinfusion, short cooling flushes, strong milk capacity, pump noise, warm-up, service questions and whether a dual boiler would fit better.
- Longer-term anecdotes are useful inspection prompts but have no reliable denominator or consistent technician diagnosis.

This community evidence supplies bounded owner questions only. Unknown evidence includes failure incidence, remaining service life, measured temperature, measured noise, measured steam performance and the condition of any unit not inspected by Coffeedant; those points remain unknown.
