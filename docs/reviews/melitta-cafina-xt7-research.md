# Melitta Cafina XT7 research ledger

Checked September 24, 2026. This ledger supports `/espresso-machine/melitta-cafina-xt7/` and permanent product ID `CD-EM-000209`. The assessment is research-led. Coffeedant did not operate, install, taste from, measure, open or durability-test this machine.

## Exact identity and regional boundary

The [current Melitta Professional US page](https://www.melitta-professional.com/us/en/coffeemachines/fuer-den-praktischen-comfort/Melitta-Cafina-XT7) says Melitta North America offers the XT7 only as a two-step version. It describes an automatic Steam Perfect/Steam Control Plus wand so staff can froth separately while espresso brews.

The [current international page](https://www.melitta-professional.com/int/en/coffeemachines/fuer-den-praktischen-comfort/Melitta-Cafina-XT7) instead positions the XT7 around integrated TopFoam, cold foam, two milk types and Steam Control Plus. These are materially different milk workflows. The review does not import the international one-step milk specification into a US purchase.

The signed schedule and rating plate control grinder count, bean paths, milk system, instant modules, cooler, power, water mode, payment, telemetry, warranty and service. XT6, XT8, CT8, older regional packages and used machines are outside this identity boundary.

## Official evidence

The current US page lists 400 V / 5.7 kW and a 300 × 580 × 715 mm body. The drink-specific DIN 18873-2 figures are:

- café crème: 135 single / 196 paired;
- espresso: 176 single / 300 paired;
- cappuccino: 165 single / 280 paired;
- latte macchiato: 150 single / 210 paired;
- cocoa: 80;
- hot water: 35 litres;
- daily output: 250.

Melitta conditions those figures on equipment, water connection, vessel size and quality settings. They are standardized capacity figures, not Coffeedant measurements of a complete queue, beverage mass, extraction, temperature or cup quality. A US two-step milk drink adds staff work that is not captured by coffee dispense alone.

The shared architecture includes a stainless brewer, micro-fine sieve, up to 20 g dose, ACS quality correction, VPS pressure control, an 8.4-inch touch display, waiter and self-service modes, CIP cleaning and optional Insights telemetry. The [milk-systems overview](https://www.melitta-professional.com/us/en/coffeemachines/technologiekompetenzen/milchsysteme) distinguishes Basic, Standard, Professional and TopFoam systems; names do not substitute for a signed configuration.

## Cleaning and service

Melitta’s [hygiene record](https://www.melitta-professional.com/int/en/coffeemachines/technologiekompetenzen/hygiene) explains in-place coffee and milk path cleaning. CIP does not remove operator duties. Staff still handle approved agents and containers, the delivered milk equipment, refrigeration, waste, outlet contact areas and prompts.

The US two-step wand routine and an international integrated TopFoam routine are different. The dealer must demonstrate the exact delivered cleaning process, assign shift responsibility and include it in site acceptance.

## Independent evidence

[BeanScene](https://www.beanscenemag.com.au/melitta-cafina-xt7/) published a May 10, 2021 trade profile describing the 8.4-inch interface, ACS and a stated output of up to 170 cups per hour. It does not disclose controlled test methods, matched recipes or long-term operation, so it is treated as an independent publisher profile rather than a measured review.

[Coffee Republic](https://www.coffeerepublic.com.au/automatic-coffee-machine/melitta-cafina-xt7) publishes Australian configuration, utility and featured-site material. Its hourly table differs from Melitta’s current DIN figures. The review therefore preserves the conflict and uses the current manufacturer DIN record for specification.

The [archived UK brochure](https://caffemat.com/data/userfiles/files/Brochure%20Melitta%20Cafina%20XT7%20UK.pdf) corroborates 400 V / 5.7 kW, compact dimensions, ACS and regional milk options, but it is older than the current US page and does not define the North American package.

No independent controlled dataset was located for beverage temperature, extraction, dose accuracy, sound, installed energy, rinse water, waste, full-drink queue speed, cleaning labour, uptime, component life or total cost.
