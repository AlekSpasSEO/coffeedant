## Official evidence

The current Bezzera Unica page, official brochure, and January 2026 parts catalog establish a 0.5 L single boiler, 3 L reservoir, vibration pump, manual thermosyphon group, AISI 304 body, and shared steam and hot-water wand. Bezzera documents a PID range of 80–100°C for coffee and 105–120°C for steam, dimensions of 250 × 425 × 375 mm, 18.5 kg mass, and regional electrical ranges. The PID changes one boiler between jobs; it does not create simultaneous brewing and steaming.

## Editorial decisions

The article treats the Unica as an espresso-first traditional single boiler. The PID and group support a strong coffee score, while the half-liter boiler and serial milk transition lower milk and workflow. Value is based on specifically wanting this group and ownership style, not on assuming all PID machines are equivalent.
