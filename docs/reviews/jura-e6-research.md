## Identity and official evidence

The review covers the current North American E6 Dark Inox NAC, article 15621. Official JURA product, shop and NAC manual records control the identity. They support 11 specialties, ten strength levels, a 5 to 16 g brewing unit, Professional Aroma Grinder, HP1 Easy Cappuccino milk system, 2.8-inch color display with six buttons, optional Wi-Fi Connect, J.O.E. compatibility, 1450 W power, two-year warranty, 11 by 13.8 by 17.6 inch cabinet and about 21 lb weight. The official US shop showed $1,899 and in-stock status on the check date.

The official image delivery path retains an older EC article reference. That filename is not used as specification evidence. Current NAC 15621 documentation controls, and the media caption discloses the family-image boundary.

## Independent, community and unknown evidence

A June 3, 2026 independent C9, E6 and E8 comparison and a December 7, 2021 E6 review supply visible workflow and comparative context. Older-generation details are not promoted to current specifications. Community discussions from owners and prospective buyers identify useful questions about grinder adjustment, drink volume, temperature expectations, rinse water, milk-hose cleaning and service. They are self-selected and cannot establish a failure rate.

Unknown evidence includes controlled beverage temperature, extraction yield, noise, energy use in a defined protocol, long-term defect incidence and expected repair cost. Coffeedant did not test those values. The review treats JURA's automatic cleaning claims as guided maintenance, not maintenance-free ownership.
