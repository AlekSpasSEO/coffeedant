# Breville Barista Touch BES880 research ledger

Checked September 15, 2026. Target: current US Breville Barista Touch BES880 / BES880BSS, product CD-EM-000122, canonical route `/espresso-machine/breville-barista-touch-bes880bss/`. Research-led assessment only, with no Coffeedant physical test.

## Identity, lifecycle and price

- Breville US lists BES880 with an add-to-cart control at $999.95. The machine is current.
- This is the original Barista Touch, not the BES881 Barista Touch Impress. BES880 has automatic milk but leaves distribution and tamping manual. BES881 adds assisted dosing and tamping.
- The correct category is assisted semi-automatic espresso machine with integrated grinder. Coffee is ground into a removable 54 mm portafilter, so “automatic” marketing does not make it a bean-to-cup super-automatic.
- Current US facts include 30 external grinder settings, ThermoJet with a claimed three-second heater-ready time, 54 mm portafilter with 18 g target, Auto MilQ from 104°F to 167°F with eight texture settings, up to eight personalized drinks and sequential espresso and steam.

## Disagreements and boundaries

- Independent sources agree that good espresso is possible, while differing on how effortless and consistent it is. The review separates brew capability from timed dosing and manual puck variation.
- Three-second readiness is kept as Breville's heater claim, never total drink time or proof that a cold portafilter is thermally ready.
- Auto MilQ is described as automatic temperature and texture, not automatic jug fill, positioning, pouring or cleaning.
- Official dimensions are retained with Breville's stated W × D × H axes and a fit-verification warning because the displayed order is unusual.
