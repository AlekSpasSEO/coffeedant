# Mazzer Philos I200D US research ledger, October 22, 2026

The [official US Philos shop](https://shop-us.mazzer.com/products/philos) lists selectable 64 mm stainless I200D and I189D flat burrs, a 60 g hopper and canister, 6-micron stepped ring convertible to stepless, front-access cleaning, 400 W motor, 12.5 kg mass and dimensions 153 × 351 × 361 mm. This review evaluates I200D. [Mazzer's official FAQ](https://www.mazzer.com/faq-b2c-mazzer/) describes its clearer light-to-medium target and the fuller I189D traditional espresso target; those are manufacturer taste intentions, not Coffeedant tasting. A US retailer explicitly lists [120 V / 60 Hz](https://dipacciusa.com/products/mazzer-philos-light-commercial-single-dose-grinder), while the official US shop and its help page distinguish regional electrical builds. Verify the delivered nameplate. The official US page displayed unavailable on October 22, 2026 despite a catalog price, so no buy-now price is claimed.

The [official operating manual](https://www.mazzer.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/IPH0L001S-QQQ.pdf) provides broad starting dial ranges, dry cleaning and safety procedure. Independent evidence includes [WIRED's I200D one-month sample](https://www.wired.com/review/mazzer-philos-coffee-grinder/), [Tom's Guide](https://www.tomsguide.com/home/coffee-makers/mazzer-philos-review), [Bon Appétit](https://www.bonappetit.com/story/mazzer-philos-coffee-grinder-review) and a [Toms Grinder Lab three-month two-burr video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VfF8hoTc_Xc). Their coffee, burr and retention protocols differ. A low input-output difference does not prove no grounds remain or that a subsequent decaf dose has no caffeine carryover. These external tasting accounts are attributed to their testers only.

Community context includes a [one-year owner report](https://www.reddit.com/r/espresso/comments/1pgailu/year_in_review_with_the_mazzer_philos/) and a [recent I189D owner account](https://www.reddit.com/r/espresso/comments/1wvnwf9/review_conical_grinders_to_189d_flat_burrs_on_the/). They are self-selected and cannot establish a population reliability rate; I189D impressions cannot become I200D findings. Unknown: current legitimate US selected-burr stock, delivered total, exact warranty terms, long-run defect incidence and a shared blinded sensory comparison. Coffeedant has not owned, ground with, measured or tasted from the Philos. The six scores are editorial judgments with these limits.
