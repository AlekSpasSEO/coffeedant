## Editorial limits

- Coffeedant did not operate a BZ10, measure water temperature, pressure or steaming time, taste espresso, perform a cooling flush, adjust boiler pressure, or inspect internal construction.
- Suggested dose and yield language is editorial starting guidance, not a measured BZ10 result or basket guarantee.
- The dual gauge is not presented as direct brew-temperature or extraction-quality measurement.
- Simultaneous brewing and steaming is an architectural capability, not a promise of automatic milk skill or unlimited household throughput.
- Exact warmup, cooling-flush volume, cup clearance, current parts lead time, technician travel, box contents and long-run reliability remain unknown.
- Internal electrical, boiler-pressure and pump work is directed to qualified service.
