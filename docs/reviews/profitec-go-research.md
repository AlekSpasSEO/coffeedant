# Profitec GO original US research ledger

Research checked September 24, 2026. The published review is research-led; Coffeedant did not test, measure, photograph or disassemble this machine.

## Identity and generation

- Scored identity: original-generation Profitec GO sold by current US specialists for 115 V service.
- Excluded identity: Profitec GO 2.0, introduced in Europe in June 2026 with OLED controls, programmable pre-infusion, timed dosing, last-shot recall, cleaning program and automatic post-steam cooling.
- Whole Latte Love documents a 2025 original-GO running change: non-red colors receive a quieter reservoir lid and larger BPA-free plastic tray; red is excluded in its checked listing.
- Buyers must confirm generation, color, rating plate and actual tray because older stock and photographs can differ.

## Specification record and conflicts

- PID-controlled brass single boiler, 58 mm ring group, vibration pump, three-way solenoid, shot timer, pressure gauge and externally accessible OPV.
- Manufacturer dimensions: 210 × 362 × 381 mm without portafilter; 210 × 410 × 381 mm with portafilter. Weight: about 12.9 kg.
- Official product record: US 115 V and 900 W.
- Specialist records conflict between 0.3 and 0.4 L boiler volume, 2.8 and 3.0 L reservoir capacity, and 900 and 1200 W. The review does not merge those cells and tells buyers to verify the physical rating plate.
- The original GO has no programmable automatic pre-infusion, volumetric stop, dedicated hot-water outlet or simultaneous brew and steam operation.

## Dated US availability

- Clive Coffee: $1,199, Add to Cart, black, red, blue and yellow displayed.
- Whole Latte Love: $1,199 and three-year parts-and-labor warranty displayed; stock varies by selected configuration.
- The European GO 2.0 price is not used as a US original-GO benchmark.

All prices and stock states above were checked September 24, 2026. They can change and do not establish a normal long-term market price.

## Independent and community evidence

- Coffee Kev’s original-GO hands-on review supplies attributed espresso, PID offset, steaming and workflow context. Its embedded exact-generation video was published September 28, 2025. Affiliate and coffee-sales interests are disclosed.
- CoffeeGeek published an independent first look on February 19, 2025.
- Kaffeemacher purchased its GO 2.0 sample and published testing at the June 2026 launch. It is used only to define the successor boundary; its features and measurements are not transferred to the original GO.
- Longer-term Reddit discussions praise espresso control and compact build while raising steam, rattle, button and version questions. These are inspection prompts, not incidence rates.

This community evidence supplies bounded owner questions only. Unknown evidence includes failure incidence, remaining service life, measured temperature, measured noise and the condition of any unit not inspected by Coffeedant; those points remain unknown.
