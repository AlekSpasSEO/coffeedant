## Official evidence

Casa V is a legacy ECM single-boiler machine. The manufacturer manual and service material govern safe use, while exact retailer records identify the compact ring group, 58 mm portafilter, vibration pump, front pump-pressure gauge, adjustable expansion valve, roughly 2.8 L top-fill tank, about 210 × 370 × 380 mm dimensions, and approximately 14 kg mass. It uses thermostat control and has no PID display. Small boiler-capacity figures conflict among surviving records, so the article reports the approximate range and preserves the uncertainty.

## Independent evidence

Atlas Power Coffee, CoffeeRoasters, and Whole Latte Love provide surviving exact-machine specifications, photographs, setup, extraction, steaming, and maintenance context. Atlas listed NZ$1,750 at the evidence lock. These commercial records are necessary because the model is no longer in ECM's current product range. They do not prove the condition or output of any remaining-stock or used unit.

The Casa V sample is small and used conditions differ. Community reports identify inspection and ownership topics but cannot produce a defect rate or predict one machine's life.

## Editorial decisions

The review rewards 58 mm preparation, compact width, gauge feedback, and conventional mechanics, then discounts the lack of PID, serial milk workflow, and legacy risk. Classika PID is the current ECM step, Profitec GO the modern compact PID route, and a used Casa V is recommended only after exact-unit verification.
