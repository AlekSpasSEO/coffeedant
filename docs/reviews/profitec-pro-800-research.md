# Profitec Pro 800 current-generation research ledger

Research checked September 24, 2026. The published review is research-led; Coffeedant did not test, measure, photograph, service or disassemble this machine.

## Identity and lifecycle

- Scored identity: current Profitec Pro 800, item CM500012-00, with American-walnut handles, pine-green boiler gauge, lever steam and hot-water valves, and a hidden PID.
- Official manual revision: February 2022. Whole Latte Love describes the visual and valve changes as the 2022 update.
- Excluded identity: earlier rotary-knob machines, non-US electrical packages, owner-added group gauges and undocumented modifications.
- Current status: Profitec and US specialist Clive both listed the model on September 24, 2026.

## Specification and safety record

- Dipper one-circuit architecture with a 7.8 kg spring-lever group and 3.5 L copper boiler.
- PID regulates boiler temperature; the display is hidden behind the drip tray. ECO mode is programmable.
- 2.8 L reservoir or fixed water. The vibration pump fills from the tank and is not used with a fixed-water supply.
- Official fixed-water operating pressure is 2 to 3 bar. Regulation, suitable water treatment, shutoff and leak-safe installation matter.
- Drain connection is supported. The drip tray holds about 1.25 L when not drained.
- Dimensions are 339 × 505 × 492 mm without lever handle and portafilter, or 339 × 593 × 740 mm with portafilter and lever raised. Weight is 35.5 kg.
- Power is 115 V / 1,500 W in the United States and 230 V / 1,600 W in the named 230 V markets.
- The manual says a brew cannot be aborted. The portafilter must stay locked until the lever returns to neutral and residual pressure is gone.

## Dated price and package

- Clive listed the current Pro 800 at $3,299 on September 24, 2026.
- The official delivery set includes single-spout, double-spout and bottomless portafilters; 7 g, 14 g and 21 g baskets; tamper; brush; plumbing hose; drain container; and manual.
- Clive listed a three-year parts-and-labor warranty. Regional warranty and bundle terms must be checked with the seller.
- Total installed cost can include grinder, scale, water testing and treatment, pressure regulator, shutoff, drain work, freight, tax and service access.

## Independent, owner and community evidence

- The Real Sprometheus published the exact-model video `Profitec Pro 800 | Do Levers Do It Better?` on December 5, 2025. It includes overview, usability and downside sections.
- Coffee Ryokou describes two years of ownership, including the single-boiler link between espresso temperature and steam pressure. The author's pressure experimentation remains anecdotal rather than a Coffeedant measurement.
- Home-Barista revisited the 2022 generation and discussed plumbing-pressure and workflow details.
- Recent Reddit reports repeatedly mention quiet extraction, forgiving cups, substantial steam, warm-up planning, large installed size, lever technique and the difficulty of recovering from a choked shot.
- One recent three-month owner report describes serving more than ten drinks at a gathering. It is not a commercial-throughput test.

No owner thread supplies a controlled denominator for failure incidence or expected service life. Grinder, water, roast, boiler setting, pre-infusion, plumbing pressure and modifications differ.

This community evidence supplies bounded fit and inspection questions only. Unknown evidence includes failure incidence, remaining service life, measured warm-up, measured puck temperature, measured noise and the condition of any unit Coffeedant has not inspected; those points remain unknown.

## Claims deliberately not made

- No Coffeedant access, tasting, pressure, temperature, noise, steam, warm-up or extraction-yield measurement.
- No universal flavor promise, steaming time, group-ready time, reliability rate or lifespan.
- No claim that boiler PID temperature equals puck temperature.
- No claim that the Pro 800 is a dual boiler. Profitec identifies it as a dipper one-circuit system.
- No claim that retailer pressure or temperature statements transfer to every installation.
- No claim that an owner-added group gauge, spring-resistance technique or different basket is factory equipment.
- No claim that the checked US price will persist after September 24, 2026.
