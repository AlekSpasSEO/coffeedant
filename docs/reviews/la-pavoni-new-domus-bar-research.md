## Identity and generation boundary

The current official EU page and exact sheet name stainless `LPCDMB02EU`. The sheet was generated September 24, 2026. Older `DMB` listings and US `LPCDMB02US` material can specify 1200 W, a 57 mm holder and a brass boiler. Those are not treated as specifications for the current EU machine.

## Exact official facts adopted

- Stainless 0.25 L single boiler and chromed-brass brew group.
- 58 mm internal filter-holder diameter on the exact EU page and sheet.
- Vibration pump with 15-bar component rating and a pump manometer. The rating is not presented as pressure at the puck.
- 2.7 L removable tank with water-level indicator.
- Integrated grinder with 38 mm conical burrs and a 130 g bean hopper.
- Manual portafilter-activated grinder switch and manually stopped brewing.
- Passive cup warmer and one stainless wand for steam and hot water.
- 300 × 250 × 345 mm, 10.5 kg net and 1.6 m cord.
- Double-spout holder, one-cup, two-cup and paper-pod filters, ABS tamper and scoop.

## First-party grinder conflict

La Pavoni's current introduction calls the grinder seven-position adjustable. The manual displays seven numbered references around the adjustment dial and refers to positions. The same current product page lists stepless micrometric adjustment under functions. The exact product sheet identifies the 38 mm conical burrs and 130 g hopper but does not resolve adjustment style. The review states this conflict and advises checking the delivered unit.

## Price and regional boundary

## Community evidence boundary

A Reddit owner of an older Domus reported that a Lelit 57.35 mm levelling tool fit. That does not override the current EU 58 mm specification. The owner video shows the practical manual sequence. These records inform decision questions, not failure rates or universal recipes.

## Editorial conclusion

The current EU New Domus Bar is best understood as a compact manual station, not a guided hybrid. Its case is a 58 mm holder, pump gauge, large tank and integrated grinder in one narrow cabinet at €831. Its limits are the tiny single boiler, manual dose and shot control, constrained grinder upgrade path and unresolved first-party adjustment description.
