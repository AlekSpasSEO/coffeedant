## Exact identity and region

The [Krups UK product page](https://www.krups.co.uk/ESPRESSO-MACHINES/BEAN-TO-CUP-MACHINES/Arabica-Digital-Bean-to-Cup-Coffee-Machine---Black/p/8010000001) identifies the black EA817040, not the silver EA817840, carbon EA817K40 or Arabica Manual EA811040/EA811840. Its UK specification is 220–240 V, 50 Hz and 1450 W. The manufacturer gives 245 W × 365 D × 330 H mm, a 1.7 L water tank, three grinding levels, three temperature levels, two favourites and a manual steam nozzle. Its promotional text describes 250 g bean capacity, while its specification table says 260 g. The review preserves that disagreement instead of selecting an invented precise hopper size. The page advertises two cups; the [official EA815–EA817 instruction manual](https://dam.groupeseb.com/m/e394ee30f0322ff8/original/8080014573-IFU.pdf?timestamp=20250714200216) explains sequential black-coffee cycles and separate manual milk frothing. The manual's 2022 family scope is disclosed; the live exact-model page controls the variant identity.

The manual describes automatic priming, programmed ristretto/espresso/coffee volumes, a cleaning-tablet program, descaling prompts, steam-nozzle purging and manual cleaning. It warns that steam does not cease immediately after stop. The official [EA817040 support and FAQ](https://www.krups.co.uk/instructions-for-use/Products-/Beverages/Full-automatic/Arabica-Digital-Bean-to-Cup-Coffee-Machine---Black/csp/8010000001) governs owner care. Krups's 15-year repairability promise is a parts/service commitment, not a measured service life.

## Independent evaluation and owner context

[DavidInFrance Reviews' exact-model owner video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=snkES99AKdc) was published December 8, 2025; its title says three years of daily use and its description explicitly names EA817040. The creator discloses Amazon affiliate links. This is independent firsthand owner context, not a controlled laboratory test or Coffeedant access. The [Currys EA817040 overview](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hn8X3Uyu61k) is retailer-produced model-identification and visual context. [Coffeeness's hands-on review of the adjacent EA8250](https://www.coffeeness.de/en/krups-ea8108-review/) is used only to frame questions about a different Krups platform; no extraction result or specification is transferred to EA817040.

An [exact-model Reddit owner](https://www.reddit.com/r/superautomatic/comments/1991787/krups_ea817040_70_super_automatic_bean_to_cup/) reports bitter coffee despite attempted adjustments; [another owner](https://www.reddit.com/r/fixit/comments/1sxxo3s/fix_leak_of_krups_coffee_machine_ea817040/) asks about a leak. Neither establishes a failure rate, universal taste, cause of a leak or a widespread safety defect. The legacy Coffeedant assertion that the steam nozzle frequently detaches is unsubstantiated and was removed, while the documented hot-steam safety advice remains.
