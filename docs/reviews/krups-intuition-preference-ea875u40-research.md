## Exact UK article and official manufacturer evidence

The [Krups UK EA875U40 page](https://www.krups.co.uk/ESPRESSO-MACHINES/BEAN-TO-CUP-MACHINES/INTUITION-PREFERENCE%2B-Bean-to-Cup-Coffee-Machine---Dark-Grey/p/8010001185) identifies the Urban Metal article, UK plug, 15 named recipes, 3.5-inch TFT, two profiles with seven favourites each, One Touch Cappuccino x2, five manual grinder positions, three coffee temperatures, a variable 6–9 g brewing-unit listing, 3 L water, 250 g beans, nine-puck collector, 14 cm outlet and 15-bar *pump rating*. It lists automatic coffee and milk programs while the description still directs owners to rinse the milk tube after each use. No cold-drink menu or smartphone connectivity is documented. The page contradicts itself by listing both 1450 W and 1550 W; DirectVacuums says 1450 W. The review preserves that conflict and directs owners to the delivered rating plate.

The product page links the [exact instruction PDF](https://dam.groupeseb.com/m/dadfc45f92f7e9ab/original/8020008028-IFU.pdf?timestamp=20250714164208), accessories such as XS3000F0 tablets, F08801 filter, XS804000 milk pot and XS805000 tube kit, and [Krups UK warranty terms](https://www.krups.co.uk/warranty). A 15-year repairability statement is a parts/service commitment, not a 15-year guarantee or expected lifespan.

## Independent evaluation and video

Łukasz Proszek's [independent Polish EA875U test](https://www.prorankingi.pl/blog/krups-intuition-preference-ea875u-test-opinia/), updated May 31, 2026, documents setup, controls, drinks, milk, paired output and care on his unit. He liked the milk foam, found black drinks differentiated and described the interface and alerts as useful. An early paired-cup attempt was uneven, while a later repeat was more similar; he explicitly did not know whether the first result reflected the individual unit. His taste, timing and physical observations belong to that Polish unit, beans and conditions, not a confirmed UK EA875U40 plate.

The selected [PROrankingi espresso video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QeCOez3OOyI) is a 1:01 independent demonstration published October 13, 2022 and linked to the same full test. It is unique to this review and illustrates Polish EA875U workflow only. No video stills are copied.
