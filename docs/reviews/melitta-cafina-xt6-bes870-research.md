# Melitta Cafina XT6 research ledger

Checked September 24, 2026. This ledger supports `/espresso-machine/melitta-cafina-xt6-bes870/` and permanent product ID `CD-EM-000208`. The assessment is research-led. Coffeedant did not operate, install, taste from, measure, open or durability-test this machine.

## Exact identity and configuration boundary

The [current Melitta Professional US page](https://www.melitta-professional.com/us/en/coffeemachines/fuer-den-praktischen-comfort/Melitta-Cafina-XT6) identifies the product as Cafina XT6. It does not use “BES870” as an XT6 model identifier. The BES870 text is retained only in the legacy Coffeedant route so existing links continue to work; it is removed from the review title and machine identity.

The XT6 is a configured commercial family rather than one fixed retail SKU. The exact grinder count, bean paths, milk system, instant modules, cooler, water mode, payment hardware, telemetry, power, warranty and service package depend on the signed dealer schedule. This review excludes XT7, XT8, CT8, older regional builds and any particular used machine. The delivered rating plate and commissioning record control the installation.

## Official evidence

The current US and international pages list a daily output of 250, 200–240 V power, a 2.175–3.040 kW electrical range and a 300 × 580 × 715 mm body. The DIN 18873-2 hourly figures are:

- café crème: 133 single / 192 paired;
- espresso: 170 single / 300 paired;
- cappuccino: 165 single / 270 paired;
- latte macchiato: 150 single / 196 paired;
- cocoa: 80;
- hot water: 35 litres.

Melitta conditions output on equipment and settings. These are standardized capacity figures, not Coffeedant measurements of queue speed, beverage mass, temperature, extraction or cup quality.

Current functions include a stainless brewer, micro-fine sieve, up to 20 g dose, ACS automatic quality correction, VPS pressure control, an 8.4-inch touch display, waiter and self-service modes, electric outlet adjustment, CIP cleaning and optional Insights telemetry. Professional Milk may provide hot and cold foam and two milk types. Steam, instant and hot-water options vary by configuration.

Melitta’s [hygiene record](https://www.melitta-professional.com/int/en/coffeemachines/technologiekompetenzen/hygiene) explains closed-path cleaning. CIP does not remove operator work: staff still handle approved cleaning agents and containers, milk and refrigeration, waste, contact areas and prompts.

A comparable quote must include exact hardware, freight, commissioning, cooler, water treatment, payment, telemetry, cleaners, filters, training, preventive maintenance, labour, parts and response time. Site planning must verify actual voltage and amperage, water, waste, counter load, accessories and service clearance.

## Independent evidence

[VendingTests](https://vendingtests.com/test/heissgetraenkeautomaten/melitta-cafina-xt6) published an XT6 data review updated August 3, 2026. It explicitly states that the machine was not physically tested and that the review was compiled from five sources. Its summary and price range are used only as source-based independent context, not as a measured laboratory verdict.

[Coffee Republic](https://www.coffeerepublic.com.au/automatic-coffee-machine/melitta-cafina-xt6) and [Green Plantation](https://www.greenplantation.com/a/automatic-coffee-machines-from-melitta) provide secondary configuration and deployment context. Both sell coffee equipment. Coffee Republic publishes hourly figures that differ from Melitta’s current DIN table; the review defers to the current official table and makes the discrepancy explicit.

No independent controlled dataset was located for drink temperature, extraction, dose accuracy, sound, installed energy, rinse water, waste, cleaning duration, queue performance, uptime, component life or total cost.

## Community evidence

Public exact-model owner evidence is sparse. Coffee Republic publishes one named Stone & Chalk testimonial praising its service and coffee experience, plus a private-school installation snapshot describing roughly 200 daily cups for 300 staff with configured milk and instant products. Both are vendor-curated and neither supplies a matched configuration, audited operating period, failure history or independent method.

Di Pacci showed zero customer reviews when checked. An empty review count cannot support either positive or negative sentiment. No representative owner survey, fleet denominator or independently audited reliability record was located. The on-page community panel keeps these observations and limits readable inside Coffeedant without outbound links.
