## Conflicts and limits

- App support conflicts. Nivona's current Russian exact-model record says Bluetooth control is absent. Some current European specialist listings and Coffeeness say the app is supported. Region, firmware or listing quality may explain the difference; no universal claim is made.
- Memory count conflicts. Stronger exact-model records document ten My Coffee slots; House of Coffee says five. The review tells buyers to verify the delivered menu.
- Weight conflicts from about 9.4 kg to 12 kg. No single figure is promoted as exact.
- Height is listed as 33 or 34 cm. Buyers are told to allow top access beyond either body figure.
- Current seller availability does not prove that NICR 825 remains in Nivona's principal current catalog in every country.
