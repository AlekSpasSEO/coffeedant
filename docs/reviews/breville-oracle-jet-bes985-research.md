## Official evidence

Breville US anchors BES985 as Oracle Jet with automated 58 mm grind, dose, and tamp; rapid ThermoJet heating; Barista Guidance; Auto MilQ; a color touchscreen; Wi-Fi; and over-the-air update capability. It is not the independent dual-boiler architecture of BES980 or BES990. The checked base price was $1,999.95, with premium finishes up to $2,299.95.

## Independent evidence

Lifestyle Lab's September 13, 2024 video and related review show the guided preparation, extraction feedback, screen, and milk workflow. Coffeeness provides a second independent market and cup-workflow cross-check. Reviewer results remain their evidence; Coffeedant did not adopt them as controlled measurements or universal outcomes.

## Community evidence

Three exact-model Reddit records were used: a later satisfaction check, a two-month review, and an inconsistency troubleshooting thread. Together they identify praise for speed and approachability plus questions about calibration, grinder changes, milk settings, and software. They are self-selected community anecdotes and do not establish failure or satisfaction rates.

Coffeedant made no taste, heat-up, extraction, milk, noise, or network measurements. Alternative-milk results vary by formulation.

## Editorial decisions

Workflow and features receive the highest scores because guidance connects extraction behavior to an adjustment instead of merely displaying recipes. Espresso and milk also score strongly. Build and value preserve the cost of dense integration, sequential heating, and finish premiums. The recommendation favors Jet for most guidance-focused Oracle buyers while naming dual-boiler overlap as a legitimate reason to choose another generation.
