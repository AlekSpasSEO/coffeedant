## Exact identity

The reviewed machine is `PL41PLUST`, the PID-equipped Lelit Glenda with a professional 58 mm group. The exact manual identifies a 230 V, 50 Hz machine and also names `PL41PLUST/110` for 120 V, 60 Hz. `PL41PLUS` without the final T is a thermostat-controlled sibling. Anna `PL41TEM` is a different LELIT57 machine. Those models must not exchange control, group, voltage, package or support claims.

## Exact documented facts

- PID-adjusted 300 ml brass single boiler.
- 58 mm professional group and filterholder system.
- Three-way solenoid valve and documented 58 mm blind-filter backflushing.
- Front manometer for coffee-circuit pressure.
- 2.7 L removable tank with side level window.
- Multidirectional steam and hot-water wand.
- Manual shot start and stop using the pump switch.
- Factory TermoPID setting of 95°C and documented 80-130°C setting range.
- Exact manual: 230 V, 50 Hz, 1250 W, about ten-minute heating guidance, 15-bar pump rating and 9 kg.
- Exact manual dimensions: 23 × 27 × 34 cm as H × W × D.

Coffee Friend and Mutbex list about 24 × 26.5 × 36.2 cm. The discrepancy is retained. Buyers are told to measure an actual used unit and allow top tank and side valve access.

## Exact manual workflow

The manufacturer-branded revision 03 manual covers first fill, boiler priming, coffee, steam, hot water, PID adjustment and maintenance. It instructs owners to stop shots manually. For steam, select steam mode, wait for the readiness lamp and purge the wand. After steaming, open the valve, select hot water and run the pump until water exits before returning to coffee mode. This refills and resets the single-boiler water circuit.

The manual calls for weekly cleaning and backflushing weekly or every 100 coffees with a PLA180S 58 mm blind filter. It says not to wash filters or the filterholder in a dishwasher. Water treatment and chemical care should follow the exact regional manual and competent service guidance.

## Owner and community context

Selected exact-model discussions repeatedly mention the appeal of factory PID and 58 mm tools, one-to-two-drink sessions, the brew-to-steam transition and cooling after steam. A longer-term owner described needing to time the heater cycle for preferred steam. A January 2025 used owner reported a five-to-ten-second shot and difficulty texturing milk; replies first questioned grinder capability, dose and technique rather than establishing a machine fault.

These are self-selected accounts across regions, ages, grinders and care histories. They cannot establish failure rates, temperature accuracy or a universal recipe. ## Editorial conclusion

PL41PLUST still has a coherent espresso-first specification. PID, 58 mm tools, a brass 300 ml boiler, pressure feedback and a three-way valve are useful. The recommendation changes because the market changed. Exact new stock is unavailable, current catalogs omit it, archived dimensions conflict, and visible parts diagrams do not guarantee supply. It is now a condition-dependent used purchase rather than a normal current-model recommendation.
