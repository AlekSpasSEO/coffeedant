# Automatic-drip whole-bean guide — November 4, 2026

Canonical: `/coffee-beans/best-coffee-beans-for-automatic-drip/`. US whole-bean market. Research-led comparison; Coffeedant did not brew or taste any bag. No price, discount, stock, extraction measurement or relative sensory score is claimed.

## Distinct decision

The existing `/coffee-beans/best-coffee-beans-for-espresso-and-drip/` compares one bag moving between espresso and drip. This guide is for an automatic filter brewer making a shared carafe. It prioritizes explicit roaster auto-drip positioning, batch dose, household flavor direction, changing blends and first-bag size. Current site routes and aliases were checked before selecting the topic. No existing automatic-drip-only recommendation guide was found.

## Selection and exclusions

Required: exact current US whole-bean identity; current roaster statement supporting automatic drip; distinct household buyer use; existing Coffeedant full review; credited official image; no unsupported tasting rank. Selected Counter Culture Fast Forward for a nutty daily medium blend, Stumptown Holler Mountain for a citrus/caramel medium blend with a published 28 g/475 ml drip method, and Blue Bottle Bella Donovan for a darker chocolate/raspberry blend that Blue Bottle explicitly positions for automatic drip. Bella Donovan's 27 g/350 g hand-pour recipe is *not* an auto-drip machine program.

Peet's Big Bang was excluded because its current direct product listing has a sold-out ambiguity, documented in its full review. Three Africas has a roaster hand-pour recipe but no explicit automatic-drip statement in the checked product page. These exclusions avoid inventing method evidence or availability.
