# October 30 review editorial rewrite

The English review corpus contains 160 equipment reviews (159 in the registry plus the standalone Bambino Plus) and 35 bean reviews. The shared factory supplied 129 equipment reviews with long generic body extensions; the remaining 30 registry reviews already had distinct substantive analysis and were preserved.

## Changes

- Removed shared setup itineraries, brewing lessons, troubleshooting, score caveats and buying-advice padding. Product-authored findings, evidence, comparisons, exact-model boundaries and source references remain.
- Reworked Gaggia/JURA setup, daily use, care, FAQs and conclusions around the exact model. Alternative cards now explain the difference relative to the reviewed machine and avoid self-recommendations. Corrected a Velasca sentence incorrectly inherited from JURA's closed brew-unit architecture.
- Separated the Mozzafiato and Giotto decisions; replaced repeated Quick Mill care paragraphs with the relevant heating, water, access and service questions. Clarified the Philips 4300/5400 household-profile and milk-menu distinction.
- Added an individual first-bag plan and distinct conclusion to every bean review; removed generic FAQ and decaf padding. Existing source and price check dates remain unchanged. October 30 is an editorial revision date, not a new tasting or verification claim.
- Removed the equipment word quota. Required evidence, comparison, rating, accessibility and schema checks remain. Equipment retains six visible FAQs; beans require at least three relevant questions.
- Extended originality validation from titles/openings to authored body paragraphs, lists, FAQs and conclusions. Shared interface, methodology, attributed quotations and source metadata are excluded. Thirteen regression tests cover the boundary.
- Live verification also exposed old positional JURA and Keurig paragraph trimming. Removed those obsolete passes so setup, model scope and care advice survive the shorter factory; the equipment validator now rejects setup sections containing only a system specification.

## Evidence and verification

The same built-page checker found 58 widespread body-passage groups and 14 repeated long paragraphs before the rewrite. The revised corpus has zero of either and passes across all 195 reviews. Remaining shorter shared technical descriptions are expected where products share hardware; the checker does not establish semantic originality or replace editorial review.

Production-target builds, equipment/bean validators, source-reference and section-anchor checks, SEO/schema tests and desktop/mobile inspection accompany the change. The Amazon API pause remains in effect; no credentials, refreshes, probes or API requests were used.
