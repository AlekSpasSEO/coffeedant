# La Marzocco Linea Mini research ledger

Review route: `/espresso-machine/la-marzocco-linea-mini/`
Product ID: `CD-EM-000060`
Model scope: the redesigned current US Linea Mini, commonly called Linea Mini R by dealers and reviewers, not an original-generation used Mini.
Price check: September 15, 2026. La Marzocco USA listed the configurable machine at $6,600 before grinder, water treatment, or installation.

## Official evidence

1. [La Marzocco USA Linea Mini](https://home.lamarzoccousa.com/product/linea-mini/) establishes current price, ordering status, dimensions, boiler capacities, electrical specification, reservoir, included accessories, and app-connected feature set.
2. [La Marzocco USA home-machine warranty](https://home.lamarzoccousa.com/warranty/) states 24 months of parts and labor for the Linea Mini, subject to the published terms.
3. [La Marzocco USA technical support](https://www.lamarzoccousa.com/support/) provides the manufacturer contact and service path.
4. [Linea Mini R parts catalog](https://home.lamarzoccousa.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/Mini_R_Parts_-Catalog_V1.0-1.pdf) identifies current assemblies and supports service planning. It is not a blanket authorization for owner repair.
5. [Official app connection guide](https://home.lamarzoccousa.com/connect-the-linea-mini-to-the-la-marzocco-app/) supports the pairing and connected-control claims.

## Independent evidence

Kaffeemacher's technical review, DOWNTOWN's daily-use assessment, Coffee Blog's long-form review, Clive Coffee's hands-on overview, and CoffeeGeek's earlier-generation technical account were compared. The current-generation sources support the capacity, steam, app, fit, and workflow discussion. Earlier Mini evidence is used only where the generation difference is explicit. Stillwater Coffee's Mini-versus-Micra comparison is used to frame household throughput and counter-space value, not as manufacturer specification.

Selected video: [Artisti Coffee Roasters, La Marzocco Linea Mini R Coffee Machine Review (Everything You Should Know)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vQlOrcT-9co), published May 2, 2024. This is a commercial demonstration of the redesigned machine. Its use observations remain attributed to the creator.

## Community evidence

Home-Barista's owner-perspective thread, r/LaMarzocco maintenance and life-expectancy discussions, and the r/espresso value debate provide distinct owner pools. They are useful for questions about water, preventative service, high-volume use, grinder pairing, Micra alternatives, and emotional value. They do not establish average failure rates or a guaranteed service life.

## Unknowns and disagreements

- Population-level reliability, expected lifespan, annual service cost, and repair incidence remain unknown.
- App features and firmware behavior can change after this review date.
- Plumb-in installation cost, water-treatment needs, and local service logistics depend on the home and region.
- Some independent accounts cover the original Linea Mini. Those observations cannot be treated as current Mini R component specifications.
- The value difference versus Linea Micra depends on actual drink volume and steam demand, not the brand badge alone.
