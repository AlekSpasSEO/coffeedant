## Official evidence

The exact De'Longhi Philippines listing, global product page, and support material identify ECAM220.22.GB as the manual-frother Magnifica Start. Its four functions are steam, espresso, coffee, and Americano. Published details include a 250 g hopper, 1.8 L water tank, fourteen-serving grounds capacity, 140 mm cup clearance, 240 × 440 × 350 mm body, 8.8 kg mass, and 1450 W rating. The ₱68,800 price is a regional direct-store reference, not a global valuation.

## Independent evidence

Coffee Blog and Mr.Coffee Reviews provide third-party context for the simple icon panel, bean-to-cup routine, manual milk step, grinder adjustment, drink strength, rinsing, and value. They do not authorize transferring automatic LatteCrema behavior from ECAM220.60 or ECAM220.80 variants. Coffeedant did not reproduce their drinks or measurements.

## Community evidence

The cited r/superautomatic manual-frother thread and Magnifica comparison thread highlight questions about water discharge before steam, wand position, milk aeration, grinder movement, family naming, and whether a wider recipe menu is worth paying for. These are self-selected accounts across related ECAM220 and Magnifica machines. They are used to frame checks, not to calculate reliability or milk-quality rates.

## Unknown or unresolved

Long-term exact-suffix durability, brew dose, measured temperature, sound, flow, steam dryness, milk texture, grinder retention, repair frequency, regional accessories, and current stock remain unknown. The practical gap between a local manual-frother Start and a discounted automatic-milk sibling changes by market. Coffeedant has no hands-on taste, timing, noise, or durability evidence.

## Editorial decisions

The review fixes the identity to ECAM220.22.GB and treats manual milk as both a lower-complexity advantage and a workflow cost. It scores black-coffee use more strongly than milk use, excludes profiles and screen features, and recommends Magnifica Evo or Plus only when their larger menus or automatic milk solve a real daily need.
