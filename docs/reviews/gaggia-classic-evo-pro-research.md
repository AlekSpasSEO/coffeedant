## Identity and region

This review is deliberately limited to the North American 2023 RI9380/46 Classic Evo Pro generation with a factory-coated aluminum boiler. It is discontinued and is not the later uncoated transition version, brass-boiler E24 or dual-boiler GT.

## Current market evidence

The $499 figure is a documented 2023 launch reference, not a current new-stock offer. A used or old-stock buyer must verify rating plate, boiler history, service records, voltage, condition and warranty individually.

## Evidence decisions

- The official 2023 US manual controls operation, safety and exact model identity.
- CoffeeGeek and Lifestyle Lab provide dated independent evaluation and visible workflow context.
- Coating complaints are treated as a used-buying inspection and service issue. Self-selected reports do not establish a defect rate.
- The current E24 product page is used only as successor context, never as proof that the 2023 machine has a brass boiler.

## Media and rights

Current official Classic family media illustrates the shared exterior and workflow. Captions explicitly state that exterior images do not establish the internal boiler revision.

Evidence classes remain explicit: independent evidence supplies dated evaluation, community evidence supplies inspection questions, and unknown evidence such as failure incidence stays unknown.
