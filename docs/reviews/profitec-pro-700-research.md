# Profitec Pro 700 final-generation research ledger

Research checked September 24, 2026. The published review is research-led; Coffeedant did not test, measure, photograph, service or disassemble this machine.

## Identity, lifecycle and official evidence

- Scored identity: final North American Pro 700 generation with black gauges, front PID and timer, sprung valves, rotary pump, reservoir or direct-water selection and later Fast Heat Up behavior.
- Excluded identity: pre-2019 machines, undocumented regional electrical packages and modified units whose installed hardware cannot be verified.
- Optional configurations: flow-control kit, wood accents, water-treatment components and some plumbing or drain parts. They are not universal base equipment.
- Whole Latte Love now calls its Pro 700 page an old product listing. The current Profitec product index does not list Pro 700.
- Profitec DRIVE is the current successor and adds integrated flow control, newer pre-infusion tools and OLED feedback.

## Specification and workflow record

- Separate 0.75 L stainless brew boiler and 2.0 L stainless steam boiler with independent PID control.
- 58 mm E61 lever group, automatic shot timer, brew-pump and steam-boiler gauges, adjustable expansion valve and switchable steam boiler.
- About 3.0 L removable reservoir or regulated direct water connection through a rotary pump.
- Approximately 13.4 W × 18.7 D × 16.5 H inches without the portafilter and roughly 68 to 70 lb.
- Direct plumbing can provide line-pressure pre-infusion. Reservoir operation does not create the same initial water-pressure condition.
- Flow control appears on many retailer and owner examples but remains optional unless the exact group hardware and invoice prove inclusion.

## Dated availability and price

- Whole Latte Love marked the product page as an old listing on September 24, 2026.
- A unique owner video published June 17, 2022 described the machine as a $3,000 purchase class.
- Therefore about $3,000 is recorded only as historical US context, not as a current market or checkout price.
- Any used, open-box or remaining-stock offer needs unit-specific revision, condition, scale, service, warranty, plumbing parts and seller-protection checks.

## Independent and community evidence

- Homegrounds provides independent editorial coverage of the dual boilers, rotary pump, plumbing, E61 workflow and optional profiling.
- A Home-Barista owner published a three-year ownership review on February 9, 2022.
- Owner discussions repeatedly value quiet rotary pumping, plumbing convenience, separate boiler control and steam capacity.
- Recurring cautions include E61 warm-up, counter depth, water treatment, manual stopping, optional flow-control calibration and revision confusion.

No owner thread supplies the denominator or controlled protocol needed for a failure rate. No modified machine defines the base configuration.

## Claims deliberately not made

Unknown evidence includes final-generation failure incidence, expected lifespan, current serial-specific parts stock, measured noise, measured warm-up, measured steam recovery and the condition of any unit Coffeedant has not inspected.

- No Coffeedant access, tasting, pressure, temperature, noise, steam or heat-up measurement.
- No reliability rate, lifespan or defect incidence.
- No claim that a historical $3,000 reference represents current new-stock availability.
- No transfer of pre-2019 behavior or regional electrical details to the final North American generation.
- No claim that optional flow control or plumbing accessories are present on every unit.
- No claim that boiler-ready means the E61 group and puck path are fully ready.
