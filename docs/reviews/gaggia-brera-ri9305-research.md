## Disagreements and unknowns

- TechGearLab called the espresso good rather than exceptional; Coffeeness is more positive at the entry price. The article frames the ceiling as credible convenience coffee.
- Panarello milk is described as easy by positive reviewers and airy by TechGearLab. Both fit an aeration-assist sleeve.
- Retailer pages have carried contradictory access specifications. Official diagrams and product photos support front tank and grounds handling plus side brew-group removal.
- No credible public data establishes a failure rate. Individual long-use and repair reports remain anecdotal.
- Seller warranty, exact suffix and stock must be checked at purchase.
