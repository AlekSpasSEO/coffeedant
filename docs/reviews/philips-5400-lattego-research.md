# Philips 5400 LatteGo EP5447/94 research ledger

Research checked September 24, 2026. The published review is research-led; Coffeedant did not test, measure, photograph or disassemble this machine.

## Identity and region

- Scored identity: US Philips 5400 LatteGo EP5447/94.
- Philips’ exact US support page explicitly marks EP5447/94 discontinued but still provides current documentation, troubleshooting, cleaning and parts access.
- Regional EP5447/90 and other 5400 codes are not treated as interchangeable.
- Philips’ archived US product page unexpectedly prints 230 V and 50 Hz. Seattle Coffee Gear’s exact US EP5447/94 record says 120 V, 1400 W and 10 A. The review discloses the conflict and directs buyers to verify the physical rating plate.

## Official specification record

- Twelve fresh-bean coffee recipes: espresso, coffee, Americano, cappuccino, latte macchiato, flat white, café au lait, caffe latte, caffè crema, ristretto, espresso lungo and travel mug.
- Milk froth and hot water are additional outputs.
- Four user profiles plus Guest; five aroma strengths; three temperature settings; adjustable coffee and milk length.
- 12-position ceramic grinder, preground bypass, 275 g hopper, 1.8 L front-access tank, 0.26 L LatteGo and 12-serving waste container.
- 246 × 372 × 433 mm and about 8 kg.
- Double-cup coffee service is supported; milk double-cup service is not.
- Removable brew group, guided descaling, AquaClean compatibility and dishwasher-safe LatteGo and tray parts.

## Dated US availability

- Espresso Machine Experts refurbished EP5447/94: $769.99 displayed in stock.
- The same retailer’s collection page displayed an $869.99 open-box exact model.
- Walmart restored EP5447/94: $549, out of stock, sold and shipped by Seattle Coffee Gear, with 90-day returns displayed.
- Philips current official 5500 EP5544/95: $1,299.99, used only as successor context.
- A refurbished Philips 4300 EP4347/94 was $579, used as the lower-menu comparison.

All prices and stock states above were checked September 24, 2026. They can change and do not establish a normal long-term market price.

## Independent and owner evidence

- Coffeeness’s hands-on Philips 5400 family review supplies attributed espresso, foam, interface and maintenance observations. Its settings and sample do not predict another refurbished exact US unit.
- Espresso Canada’s exact EP5447/94 video was published January 20, 2021. It shows the control interface, recipes and LatteGo workflow. The publisher sells coffee equipment, so its commercial interest is disclosed.
- Exact-model marketplace reviews and Reddit discussions are self-selected. Recurring themes include broad drink choice, profiles and milk cleanup, plus disagreements about coffee strength, foam, rinses, refills and faults.
- Walmart’s visible 2026 reviews were incentivized verified-purchase submissions. They are not used as reliability evidence.

This community evidence supplies bounded owner questions only. Unknown evidence includes failure incidence, remaining service life, measured temperature, measured noise and the condition of any unit not inspected by Coffeedant; those points remain unknown.
