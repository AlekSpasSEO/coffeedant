## Official evidence

ECM's current product page fixes the reviewed identity to Mechanika Slim PID, also represented in official image names as Mechanika VI Slim PID. It lists an E61-style group, low-noise vibration pump, 1.9 L stainless heat exchanger, 2.8 L tank, PID display with shot counter, three boiler setpoints at 120, 124, and 128°C, selectable preinfusion, ECO mode, cleaning reminder, dual gauges, 250 × 445 × 395 mm dimensions, and 20.2 kg mass. The manufacturer 1.9 L boiler figure is retained over conflicting 2.2 L retailer copy.

## Independent evidence

Clive Coffee's $2,159 listing and Whole Latte Love's written and video material provide retail, interface, steam, size, and workflow context. The video published June 26, 2024 shows the current digital-control generation. These sources do not turn the service-boiler PID into a dedicated brew-boiler PID, and they are not Coffeedant performance tests.

## Community evidence

Two targeted r/espresso searches capture both Slim PID and VI Slim naming. Recurring questions concern generation identity, warmup, flush routine, boiler specification, steam settings, pump noise, preinfusion, width, and comparison with Mara X, Appartamento TCA, or dual boilers. Posts are self-selected and mixed by region, so they cannot establish satisfaction or fault incidence.

## Unknown or unresolved

Measured brew temperature at the puck, group recovery, steam rate, noise level, temperature effect of each preset, preinfusion flow, long-term electronics and valve life, repair cost, and current stock remain unknown. The boiler discrepancy is documented rather than averaged. Coffeedant has no physical timing, taste, measurement, or durability evidence.

## Editorial decisions

The review rewards simultaneous milk service, narrow width, timer, presets, and current support. It explicitly explains that heat-exchanger state and flushing still matter. The older Mechanika V Slim is treated as a separate legacy purchase, Synchronika II as the direct-control flagship, and compact current HX rivals as workflow alternatives.
