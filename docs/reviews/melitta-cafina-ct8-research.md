# Melitta Cafina CT8 research ledger

Checked September 24, 2026. This ledger supports `/espresso-machine/melitta-cafina-ct8/` and permanent product ID `CD-EM-000207`. The assessment is research-led. Coffeedant did not operate, taste from, measure, install, open or durability-test this machine.

## Exact identity and configuration boundary

The [current Melitta Professional US page](https://www.melitta-professional.com/us/en/coffeemachines/fuer-die-grosse-getraenkevielfalt/melitta-cafina-ct8) identifies the Cafina CT8 as a configured commercial fully automatic platform for continuous and peak service. It lists 400 V / 6.8 kW and 350 × 650 × 810 mm W×D×H. The family may use one or two grinders, Professional Milk or TopFoam, two milk types, instant product, steam, hot water, fixed water or tank operation, lockable hoppers, payment hardware and optional telemetry. This review therefore does not treat “CT8” as a single retail SKU.

The scope excludes CT8-F filter brewers, CT8plus, XT8, older regional packages and any particular used machine. The signed dealer schedule, delivered rating plate, serial number and commissioning record control configuration, warranty, voltage and parts.

## Current capacity record

Melitta’s current US table lists a daily output of 350. DIN 18873-2 hourly figures are:

- café crème: 142 single / 198 paired;
- espresso: 178 single / 300 paired;
- cappuccino: 168 single / 288 paired;
- latte macchiato: 150 single / 232 paired;
- cocoa: 80;
- steam/hot water: 40 litres.

Melitta explicitly conditions figures on equipment, water connection, vessel size and quality settings. The page’s marketing statement of up to 250 cups is not substituted for its drink-specific table. No independent queue, beverage-mass, recovery, energy, rinse-water or waste test was located.

## Coffee, milk and control architecture

Current manufacturer functions include a stainless brewing group, micro-fine sieve, Crema Nozzle, up to 20 g weighing, ACS automatic quality correction and VPS pressure adjustment. One or two grinders are configuration dependent. The 10-inch touch display supports waiter and self-service modes; double products require the delivered setup.

Milk options include Professional Milk or TopFoam with cold foam, two milk types, steam and a milk foam lance. These are not assumed standard together. Cooler, milk formulation, hose routing, recipe, sanitation and refill practice affect performance.

## Installation and price

No public current US list price was located on September 24, 2026. The current page routes buyers to project contact rather than checkout. The article therefore uses “quote required” and no invented transaction figure.

A comparable quote needs exact machine hardware, freight, commissioning, cooler, payment, telemetry, water treatment, cleaners, filters, operator training, preventive maintenance, labour rates, parts stocking and response time. The 400 V / 6.8 kW supply, water, waste, counter load and service clearances make this a facilities project rather than a home countertop purchase.

## Cleaning and service

Melitta’s [current hygiene page](https://www.melitta-professional.com/int/en/coffeemachines/technologiekompetenzen/hygiene) describes CIP as closed-system cleaning of coffee and milk paths without dismantling core components. Operators still insert cleaning agents, exchange the milk container for a cleaning container and start the program.

The [official daily cleaning guide](https://ct8.melitta-professional.com/fileadmin/Resources/Public/PDF/en/Daily%20Cleaning%20CT8%202-3-15%20Approved.pdf) shows waste-drawer emptying, cleaner and cleaning-pitcher handling. The [linked 2015 operator manual](https://ct8.melitta-professional.com/fileadmin/Resources/Public/PDF/es/Operators%20manual%20ct8.pdf) adds housing, refrigerator, milk-tube and grounds-drawer work. It directs operators to isolate power and water and contact approved service for leaks, odour, heat or error messages. The old manual is useful workflow evidence, not proof that every current regional machine has identical hardware or screens.

The report identifies a CT8 offering hot and iced lattes, cappuccino, mocha, flat white, Americano, café con leche and double espresso, with a reported 200-drink hourly capacity. This is observed deployment context, not a controlled test or a universal menu.

Both sell equipment; their claims are secondary procurement context rather than independent laboratory evidence.
