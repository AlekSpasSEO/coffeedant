# Quick Mill Carola Evo 0960-A-CEVO-A research ledger

Research checked September 24, 2026. The published review is research-led; Coffeedant did not test, taste with, measure, photograph, service or disassemble this machine.

## Identity and lifecycle

- Scored identity: Quick Mill Carola Evo, North American product ID 0960-A-CEVO-A, current front-display configuration, 110 V.
- Official manufacturer evidence establishes the current Carola as a 0.75 L PID single-boiler machine with no steam function.
- Current Quick Mill and US importer records govern the scored identity. The US machine includes a front PID display, automatic shot timer, standby control and low-water code.
- Carola became PID controlled in April 2020. Chris Coffee records additional 2021 changes to the switch, heating light, low-water behavior, drain and expansion-valve location.
- Excluded identities: pre-April 2020 Carola machines, the 2019 hidden-switch PID layout, regional 230 V units, seller ESE claims, and machines with owner-installed flow control or other modifications.
- Current status: Quick Mill publishes Carola in its current range. Chris Coffee showed US stock on September 24, 2026; two checked US peers showed the same price but no stock.

## Specification and safety record

- One 0.75 L insulated T.E.A.-coated brass boiler serves brewing only. There is no steam wand or steam mode.
- The 58 mm E61 thermosiphon group uses a manual lever, three-way brew circuit, stainless mushroom and stainless gicleur.
- PID controls the boiler setting and the display supplies a shot timer, programmable standby and low-water status. It does not measure puck temperature or beverage yield.
- The official tank record is 1.8 L; current US specialists list 2 L. This difference is preserved rather than silently harmonized.
- Pump record: 52 W Ulka vibration pump with Quick Mill's Pulsar dampener, thermal protection and an accessible expansion valve.
- North American electrical record: 110 V, 850 W and 15 A. The rating plate must be checked on every unit.
- Current US specialist dimensions are about 7.75 inches wide, 15.5 inches deep and 13.75 inches high. Weight is about 32 lb.
- Initial boiler fill must follow the current manual before heating. Hot E61 metal, hot brew water, mains electricity and residual group pressure require normal espresso-machine precautions.

## Dated price and package

- Chris Coffee displayed $1,395 and in-stock status on September 24, 2026.
- 1st in Coffee and Espresso Outlet each displayed $1,395 but showed out-of-stock status on September 24, 2026.
- Chris Coffee and 1st in Coffee described a two-year seller warranty; Espresso Outlet described one year. Warranty, freight and fulfillment remain seller-specific.
- Current package records identify single- and double-spout portafilters, baskets, blind disk and group brush, but a delivered or used unit must be checked item by item.
- A used price needs adjustments for generation, voltage, PID interface, water history, scale, pump and heater condition, E61 service, accessories, modifications, freight, tax and available technical support.

## Independent, owner and community evidence

- Kaffeemacher independently purchased and evaluated a 2019 Carola, documenting espresso, temperature behavior, counter fit, drip tray and the steam-free decision. Its hidden-switch PID interface is not transferred to the current display model.
- The independent owner video `Quick Mill Carola EVO Espresso Machine Review - E61 58MM Prosumer Home Espresso Machine with PID` was published by super_X_drifter on December 27, 2021. It covers the current-display generation, controls and espresso-only workflow.
- Home-Barista reviews from 2015 and 2018 establish earlier lineage and recurring temperature, tank, maintenance and straight-espresso questions. They do not define every current feature.
- One modified-machine owner describes an added E61 flow-control kit and reduced pump-pressure experiments. The kit, settings and results are not assigned to the factory base machine.
- A 2024 comparison discussion frames buyer priorities among Carola, ECM Classika and Profitec GO, especially milk needs, warm-up, service and pre-infusion.

No owner thread supplies a controlled denominator for failure incidence or expected service life. Generation, voltage, water, grinder, roast, warm-up, temperature setting, service history and modifications differ.

This community evidence supplies bounded fit, workflow and inspection questions only. Unknown evidence includes failure incidence, remaining service life, measured warm-up for the current generation, measured puck temperature, measured flow, measured pressure, measured noise and the condition of any unit Coffeedant has not inspected; those points remain unknown.

## Claims deliberately not made

- No Coffeedant access, tasting, temperature, pressure, flow, noise, warm-up or extraction-yield measurement.
- No claim that PID boiler setting equals puck temperature.
- No universal warm-up time, flush routine, pressure setting, flow profile, reliability rate or lifespan.
- No claim that Carola includes a steam wand, steam mode, hot-water wand, rotary pump, volumetric dosing or fixed plumbing.
- No claim that owner-installed flow control is factory equipment.
- No transfer of the 2019 hidden-switch PID offset measurement to the current front-display generation.
- No silent resolution of the official 1.8 L and US seller 2 L tank records.
- No reliability conclusion from one owner video, one modified machine or one comparison thread.
