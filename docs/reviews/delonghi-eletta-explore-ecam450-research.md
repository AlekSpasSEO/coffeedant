## Disagreements and unknowns

- Owners report materially different milk-drink temperatures. Cup preheating, recipe, milk quantity, settings, variant and unit condition can all matter. The article does not publish one universal latte temperature.
- Rapid machine “cold brew” uses low-temperature controlled flow and pressure. It is not claimed to be chemically identical to 12-to-24-hour immersion concentrate.
- Recipe counts can change by region, generation and firmware. The article dates the 50-plus EX:4 claim rather than backfilling it to all ECAM450 machines.
- App pairing and menu reports vary with software, phones and network conditions. The panel is treated as the essential baseline.
- Reviewers disagree about cold-drink intensity and whether the menu feels useful or excessive. Value is tied to repeated use of both milk systems, cold extraction, profiles and travel-mug programs.
- No public dataset establishes repair rate, app longevity, service cost or product lifespan.
- No Coffeedant temperature, noise, dose, extraction, milk-texture or long-term measurement exists.
