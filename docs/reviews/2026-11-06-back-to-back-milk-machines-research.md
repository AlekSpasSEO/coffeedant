# Best espresso machines for back-to-back milk drinks — research ledger

Scheduled and checked: 2026-11-06, Europe/Skopje. US market. Research-led; Coffeedant did not operate, time or measure these machines. No price or offer claim is carried into the guide.

## Scope and decision

Distinct from the site's generic espresso, compact, superautomatic and no-plug guides: this comparison asks which home machine can keep brew and steam work moving for several milk drinks, and whether the buyer wants manual or assisted puck preparation. Shortlist requires two separate heat circuits, current exact US variant, identifiable image provenance and an existing Coffeedant full review. A single boiler requires brew/steam switching. A heat exchanger such as the reviewed Profitec JUMP has another temperature-control routine and is linked as an alternative. Breville Dual Boiler BES920XL is omitted from primary picks because the official US listing showed out of stock; check again before a buyer treats it as available.

## Exact picks

- Lelit Elizabeth US PESEL01 on PL92T platform: [Lelit US product page](https://www.lelit.com/en-us/product/elizabeth-pesel01) states dual boilers and continuous steam without influencing coffee performance. The [technical sheet](https://www.lelit.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/EN-Elizabeth.pdf) covers the established PL92T controls. Official product image: `https://assets.breville.com/cdn-cgi/image/width=1300,format=png/Lelit/PESEL01/PESEL01_Carousel1.png` credited to Lelit US. The [September 2026 Kaffeemacher retest](https://kaffeemacher.de/en/blogs/kaffeewissen/lelit-elizabeth) is independent evidence of steaming workflow and steam-knob limitation; its individual tested machine and protocol must not be generalized to all US units. [Owner thread](https://www.reddit.com/r/Lelit/comments/1p8d9zf/lelit_elizabeth_milk_drinks_question/) asks about several drinks; responses are anecdotes, not throughput data. Full Coffeedant review: `/espresso-machine/lelit-elizabeth/`.
- Breville Oracle Dual Boiler BES995 US brushed stainless: [US product page](https://www.breville.com/en-us/product/BES995) and [feature page](https://www.breville.com/en-us/landing/oracle-dual-boiler) identify integrated grinder, assisted puck preparation and simultaneous extraction/steam. Official gallery image: `https://assets.breville.com/cdn-cgi/image/width=1200,format=png/BES995/BES995_CAROUSEL1_ANZ.png` credited to Breville US. [Tom's Guide hands-on review](https://www.tomsguide.com/home/coffee-makers/breville-oracle-dual-boiler-review) supports interface and size tradeoffs; it is not a head-to-head throughput test. Full Coffeedant review: `/espresso-machine/breville-oracle-dual-boiler-bes995/`.
- [Breville BES920 US page](https://www.breville.com/en-us/product/bes920) and [manual](https://www.breville.com/content/dam/breville/us/en/assets/miscellaneous/instruction-manual/espresso/BES920-instruction-manual.pdf) support dual-boiler capability; official US listing showed out of stock. Mention only as a conditional alternative with current authorized-stock check.
