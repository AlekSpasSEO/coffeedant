# Fellow Opus 2: research ledger

- Route: /grinder/fellow-opus-2/; ID CD-GR-000005.
- Checked October 15, 2026, Europe/Skopje. Scope is the standard matte-black US second-generation Opus 2. The original 40 mm Opus, decorative wood-accent Opus 2 variants and EU 220 V labels are separate.
- Method: research-led. Coffeedant has not bought, ground with, measured, tasted from or reliability-tested this unit. The six scores are editorial buying judgments.
- Evidence classes: **official** Fellow product listing, guide, safety manual, feature explanation and warranty; **independent** Bon Appétit and Coffee Chronicler tests plus the latter's video; **community** individual Opus 2 owner discussions; **unknown** population retention distribution, long-term impeller wear, delivered-unit behavior, actual shipment date and future support.
