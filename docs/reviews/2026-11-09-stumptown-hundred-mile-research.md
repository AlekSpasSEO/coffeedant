Slot `2026-11-09-bean-stumptown-hundred-mile`; canonical candidate `/coffee-beans/stumptown-hundred-mile/`. Coffeedant has not bought, brewed, tasted, measured or photographed this bag. No numeric sensory score or current delivered-price claim.

[Stumptown's current product page](https://www.stumptowncoffee.com/products/hundred-mile), checked November 9, names Hundred Mile as a certified organic blend, mainly East African and Central/South American coffees. It attributes the fully washed Latin American components with fruit and toffee-like sweetness and East African components with softer floral aroma; it does not name fixed farms, varieties or ratios. Its current note words are apple jam, toffee and chocolate. It positions the blend for black coffee and milk, drip, espresso and French press. Its drip recipe is 1:17; its espresso start is approximately 19.5 g in, about 1.5 fluid ounces out in 23–28 seconds. Volume output is not directly a gram yield and should not be rewritten as one. It also publishes a press routine. A $20 listing was visible, but the selected pack size/checkout was not verified; use check-price language.

[Stumptown's September 2026 blend explanation](https://www.stumptowncoffee.com/blogs/news/how-stumptown-coffee-blends-are-made) says continuing names aim for a repeatable profile while component coffees can change. It should not be read as proof of an identical current lot.

[OnlyCoffeeFans' 2026 Hundred Mile review](https://onlycoffeefans.com/stumptown-hundred-mile/) presents an easygoing chocolate/light-fruit opinion and a 9/10 subjective score, but gives little sample, bag-date or recipe detail. Do not import its score or claim a controlled tasting. [An r/Coffee owner's older bag account](https://www.reddit.com/r/Coffee/comments/ny2uch/stumptown_beans_reallyboring/) reported bitterness and a bland cup after trying several brews; another commenter preferred other Stumptown blends. This is a self-selected 2021 bag, not current quality or reliability evidence. [A separate 2025 pour-over discussion](https://www.reddit.com/r/pourover/comments/1inpxw7/fruit_and_floral_not_my_thing/) mentions Hundred Mile as acceptable with a fresher batch; also anecdotal. These mixed accounts support checking roast date and personal preference rather than a universal verdict.
