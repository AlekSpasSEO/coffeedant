## Independent measurements and interpretation

[Kaffeemacher’s Zero comparison](https://kiffeemacher.com/en/blogs/kaffeewissen/eureka-zero-vergleichstest) distinguishes temporary, permanent and absolute retained grounds, tests the exact 65 AP among other Zero models and reports nonzero retention. Its [compendium](https://kaffeemacher.ch/en/blogs/kaffeewissen/eureka-mills) reports 1.8 g absolute retention and 79.2 dB for its sample and protocol. Those measurements cannot be silently presented as US unit specifications, and they are not the same method or quantity as Eureka’s 0.55 g factory result. [DieRöster’s exact-model video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ctsbwb5RaAY) was published December 23, 2024 and is separate visual/sample context; do not attribute Kaffeemacher’s numbers to that creator. Clive’s buyer article is a seller source with commercial interest, not independent testing.

## Community accounts and unknowns

[One owner retention thread](https://www.reddit.com/r/espresso/comments/1pjs00l/eureka_mignonzero_65_ap_retention/) reports frustrating output; [another exact-model thread](https://www.reddit.com/r/espresso/comments/1hogncv/time_to_start_testing_this_mignon_zero_65_ap/) has a different single-unit account. The community sample is self-selected, small, unstandardized and not a defect or reliability rate. Unknown: carryover of regular grounds into a subsequent decaf dose under a buyer’s precise workflow, output of a given US serial unit, long-term service incidence, and individual cup preference. “Zero” is branding and a low-retention goal, not a zero-carryover certificate. A second dedicated grinder is the clearer practical option if any carryover is unacceptable.
