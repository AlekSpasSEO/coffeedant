## Identity and official evidence

The dated US market reference is JURA's factory-refurbished GIGA 6 Aluminum NAA, model 15396.99. It was out of stock at $3,499 against a stated $5,999 original price and carried a one-year refurbished warranty. This is not described as a current new-machine offer. The live UK page covers 230 V EA article 15394, so its electrical package is not substituted for NAA.

Official evidence supports two electronically adjustable ceramic grinders, Automatic Grinder Adjustment, selectable bean blending, two thermoblocks, two pumps, two fluid systems, a 4.3-inch touchscreen and HP2/CX2 milk system. The UK EA page lists 28 specialties including paired entries; the US refurb copy lists 16 individually programmable specialties. The conflict is recorded rather than reconciled with an invented explanation. Regional dimensions and capacities are separately labeled.

## Independent, community and unknown evidence

Home Grounds and the November 29, 2021 ECS Coffee video provide independent ownership and visible workflow context. Community searches identify questions about dual-grinder calibration, milk-system care, leaks, service access and the cost of discontinued parts. Self-selected posts are not used as reliability statistics.

Unknown evidence includes the condition of any individual refurbished unit, remaining parts supply, measured grinder retention, drink temperature, simultaneous-preparation time, long-term failure incidence and future service price. Coffeedant did not open, brew on or measure the GIGA 6. A buyer should obtain the exact warranty, serial record and service path in writing before purchase.
