# Londinium R24 research ledger

Checked September 24, 2026. This ledger supports `/espresso-machine/londinium-r24/` and permanent product ID `CD-EM-000205`. The review is research-led. Coffeedant did not operate, measure, open or durability-test an R24. Current first-party records control exact identity and specifications; independent video and owner evidence are labelled by role.

## Exact identity boundary

The current direct listing names `LONDINIUM R24` and SKU `LR-24V`. The product is available in 120 V and 240 V editions with regional plugs. This page does not merge the earlier Londinium R, an R2/R3 designation, Vectis, Compressa or the plumbed commercial Londinium I/II/III. Production revisions also matter: the manufacturer says boiler regulation changed to a thermistor from April 19, 2021. Rating plate, voltage, serial, delivered documentation and current support record govern an individual machine.

## Current first-party record

[The current R24 page](https://www.londiniumespresso.com/products/londinium-r24/) displayed £3,333 excluding VAT and in-stock schema on September 24, 2026. UK VAT is added at checkout. Shipping, import tax, finish, plug, voltage, portafilter choice and warranty route vary by destination. A stale page description still says “from £3,083”; the visible price and current structured offer both say £3,333, so the article uses £3,333 with the check date.

Current listed architecture:

- full-size commercial 58 mm spring group;
- heat exchanger and thermosiphon;
- app-set variable preinfusion from 1.0 to 6.0 bar;
- 24 V tank-fed rotary pump;
- 2.3 L pure copper boiler with lead-free brass fittings;
- 3.0 L reservoir and no documented direct-plumb option;
- thermistor-regulated boiler on production from April 19, 2021;
- weekly smart timer with multiple on/off events per day;
- 2400 W at 240 V and 1400 W at 120 V.

Current dimensions are 330 mm wide, 540 mm deep and 345 mm to the case top. The raised lever reaches 740 mm. The page lists 38 kg as **boxed weight**, so the article does not silently present it as net machine weight.

The standard construction description is heavy solid stainless panels, stainless drip tray and grate, solid wenge handles, and a 3 mm powder-coated zintec chassis doubled to 6 mm in high-load areas. Current inclusions are one wenge-handled portafilter chosen from the offered styles, one 16 g basket and a BWT medium softening cartridge with sleeve. A 30-minute one-to-one video training session is included. The steam arm can be specified on either side.

## Factory claims kept in proportion

Londinium says the thermosiphon and group avoid cooling and warming shots, that changing preinfusion pressure changes preinfusion pressure, brew temperature and brew volume together, that no backflushing is needed, and that the four-hole tip textures milk quickly. Those statements are attributed as maker claims. “No backflushing” describes the lever brew circuit; it does not remove shower-screen, gasket, steam, water and general care.

The maker also advertises remote support, high-definition service videos and fast parts delivery. These describe the offered service model, not a guarantee that every part will arrive everywhere in one week or that every problem is owner-serviceable. The current [support page](https://www.londiniumespresso.com/support) remains the public path to the knowledgebase, manuals and direct contact.

## Owner and community evidence

[A detailed one-year Reddit owner review](https://www.reddit.com/r/espresso/comments/17tnpcz/londinium_r24_1_year_review/) says the R24 was the owner’s first conventional espresso machine after a moka pot. That owner describes forgiving shots, strong body, ample steam, 30–40 minutes as sufficient for that routine versus a one-hour manual expectation, a useful weekly schedule and weak 2.4 GHz reception solved with a nearby extender. The owner also describes moving the cup before the spring stroke finishes and notes that manual adjustment behind the panel is inconvenient.

The long [Home-Barista R24 thread](https://www.home-barista.com/levers/londinium-r24-t65994.html) contains launch, owner and enthusiast discussion about preinfusion pressure, temperature, workflow and production changes. A separate [Home-Barista buying-advice thread](https://www.home-barista.com/advice/owners-londinium-r24-t84984.html) contains selected owner views on access, steam, support and competing levers.

[A long-term Reddit thread](https://www.reddit.com/r/espresso/comments/1l7h5is/londinium_never_disappoints/) reports daily Londinium R/R24 use since 2018. It supports a long-term owner perspective but spans generations and cannot establish a failure rate. [A critical Coffeetime thread](https://coffeetime.freeflarum.com/d/2452-londinium-r24) records one dissatisfied new owner who reported poor espresso and inadequate assistance. It is retained as counter-evidence but supplies no confirmed diagnosis or denominator.

Across these sources, repeated themes are strong perceived texture and body, quiet extraction, powerful steam, a scheduled warm-up, manual cup swapping and direct support. Clarity-focused owners do not all prefer the spring-lever style. Self-selection, grinder, coffee, water, production date, region and maintenance prevent prevalence claims.

## Claim limits

- No Coffeedant use, tasting, temperature, pressure, flow, sound, steam, energy or reliability test.
- No universal warm-up time shorter than the conservative one-hour plan.
- No defect rate inferred from owner threads.
- No promise of parts arrival, cloud uptime or local service.
- No claim that one preinfusion pressure universally matches one roast level.
- No transfer of R24 specifications to another Londinium generation.
- No interpretation of “no backflushing” as maintenance-free ownership.
