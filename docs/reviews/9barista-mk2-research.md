## Identity, lifecycle and live offer

- Exact product: 9Barista Espresso Machine Mk.2. The Standard and Pro configurations use the same brewer base. Pro changes the portafilter, basket, cap and handles.
- Region used for headline commerce: official United States storefront.
- Live evidence: the official page showed “From $750” and an order selector. Configured Pro and stove options could display a higher total.
- Electrical identity: none. It is heated by a compatible stove. Induction requires the correct selected adaptor.
- Warranty: current Mk.2 policy states five years for eligible new purchases by the original purchaser, subject to region, authorization and exclusions. Seals and safety rings are consumables.

## Disagreements and unknowns

- Published cold timing differs between product-specific and general marketing pages. The article uses the exact product page and retains the conflict.
- Original-generation reviews support the concept but cannot validate Mk.2 material and safety changes.
- Owners disagree about whether cooling and cleaning are trivial or disruptive. The difference follows drink count and safety pace.
- No public installed-base data establishes a Mk.2 failure rate or lifespan.
- Retailer bundles and secondhand warranty eligibility were not generalized.
