## Identity and generation boundary

The current official EU page and product sheet name stainless `LPSMCS01EU`. The exact sheet was generated September 15, 2026. Matte-black `LPSMCB01`, white `LPSMCW01`, US, UK and Australian electrical variants require separate checks. The newer Mini Cellini X `LPSMXS02EU`, Cellini Classic HX and Cellini Evoluzione products are not the reviewed machine.

## Exact official facts adopted

- Stainless 0.8 L single boiler without heat exchanger.
- Chromed-brass E61 group and 58 mm internal filter-holder diameter.
- Vibration pump with a 15-bar component rating. This is not presented as pressure at a coffee puck.
- 2.9 L removable tank, tank-empty alarm and no direct water connection.
- One boiler manometer, temperature light and passive cup warmer.
- Cool-touch stainless wand with steam and hot-water functions.
- 240 × 420 × 370 mm, 18 kg net and 1.8 m cord.
- Double-spout holder, single, double and blind filters, ABS tamper, scoop and brush.
- `LPAFLOWM01` is a compatible optional flow-profile kit, not included factory equipment.

## Performance boundary

Livingetc reports an in-store session with roughly eight shots on a black regional variant, consistent crema, capable steam and a separate grinder. It did not publish instrumented temperature, puck pressure, shot-yield, warm-up, sound, recovery or pitcher-time data. Coffeeness explicitly calls its article an initial review and says the machine had not yet been tested, so it is treated as a secondary preview only.

The E61 group carries substantial thermal mass even though the boiler is small. La Pavoni calls the machine quick to heat but publishes no full-group readiness time. The roughly 25-minute figure in secondary guidance is retained as non-factory workflow context, not a controlled result.

## Editorial conclusion

The Mini Cellini is best understood as a compact 58 mm E61 single boiler. Its case is traditional group hardware, a narrow steel body, a large removable tank and current exact documentation. Its trade-offs are E61 heat-soak time, brew-to-steam switching, analog temperature management, sparse feedback and the added cost of grinder, scale, proper tamper, water treatment and specialist service.
