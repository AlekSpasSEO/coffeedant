## Exact identity

The reviewed machine is `PL81T`, the Lelit Grace with LCC, a LELIT57 group, a 250 ml brass single boiler and a coffee-pressure manometer. The exact family manual names 230 V, 50 Hz and 120 V, 60 Hz regional versions. Anna `PL41TEM` is a simpler LELIT57 machine. Victoria `PL91T` has a 58 mm group and 300 ml boiler. Kate `PL82T` adds a grinder. Those models must not exchange group, boiler, control, voltage, package, firmware or support claims.

## Exact documented facts

- PID-managed 250 ml brass single boiler.
- LELIT57 group and 57 mm filterholder system.
- LCC OLED interface for coffee and steam temperature, pre-infusion, timing, standby and status.
- Later OTPGem-114 software note documents chronometer timing, reserve mode, a faster coffee-to-steam transition and dose-counter changes.
- Backlit coffee-pressure manometer.
- Three-way solenoid valve and blind-filter group cleaning.
- Multidirectional shared steam and hot-water wand.
- Manual shot start and stop.
- Exact manual: 230 V, 50 Hz and 120 V, 60 Hz regional versions, 1000 W, 15-bar pump rating and 7.4 kg.
- Exact dimensions: 22.5 × 27 × 38 cm as width × depth × height.

Tank capacity conflicts by document revision. An older manufacturer-branded manual lists 2.7 L. Revision 04 and current exact retailer archives list 2.5 L. The review retains that difference and tells buyers to check the manual and tank supplied with the unit.

## Exact manual and software workflow

The exact family manual covers first fill, LCC settings, coffee, steam, hot water, cleaning and troubleshooting. Grace remains a manual espresso machine: the user prepares the puck and starts and stops extraction. Coffee and steam share one small boiler, so a milk drink includes a rise to steam temperature, wand purge and care, boiler refill and cooling return to coffee mode.

The later software note changes the dispensing view from countdown to chronometer, adds reserve mode if the reservoir empties during a shot, improves the move to steam mode and documents dose counters. Because used units can predate that revision, the review tells buyers to verify actual firmware behavior.

## Owner and community context

Selected discussions compare Grace with Victoria and other compact PID machines. Common themes are LCC controls, small footprint, single-boiler milk pace and the long-term consequence of choosing 57 mm tools. A repair discussion found that a pump fitting in one 2018 unit did not match a published diagram. Another owner described water below the tank and electrical protection operating.

These are self-selected accounts across years, regions and maintenance histories. They cannot establish failure rates or prove a model-wide defect. They are used as inspection prompts. ## Editorial conclusion

Grace PL81T remains an unusually informative compact single boiler. PID control, programmable pre-infusion, timing, pressure feedback and the LCC make manual espresso easier to repeat. The recommendation changes because the market changed. New stock is unavailable, the current catalog omits Grace, software and tank records vary by revision, and used condition now dominates value. It is a condition-dependent used purchase rather than a normal current-model recommendation.
