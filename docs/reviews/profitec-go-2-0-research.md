## Independent and owner evidence

[Kaffeemacher's GO 2.0 purchased-sample test](https://kaffeemacher.de/en/blogs/kaffeewissen/profitec-go-im-test), published June 25 and checked November 10, separates the 2026 revision from its original GO test. The outlet reports 58.5/100 overall, with espresso 6.5/10, temperature 5.3/10, steaming 4.3/10 and accessories 9/10 under its own V2.3.2 protocol, tester and European sample/software. It calls the boiler 400 ml and tank 2.65 L; these differ from Profitec's current 0.3 L/2.8 L and the US dealer's record, so the review uses manufacturer exact specifications and explicitly notes the discrepancy. The outlet's [exact-generation video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=a_oJZGfhfF4) is a visual external test, not Coffeedant measurements. [One owner](https://www.reddit.com/r/ProfitecGo/comments/1wqujo7/hello_everyone_extremely_happy_with_my_profitec/) describes separate button workflows; [another](https://www.reddit.com/r/ProfitecGo/comments/1xdb6mg/profitec_go_20_poor_build_qualityfinishing/) reports finish concerns. These self-selected accounts have no denominator for a reliability rate. Owner claims are framed as checks for a delivered unit.
