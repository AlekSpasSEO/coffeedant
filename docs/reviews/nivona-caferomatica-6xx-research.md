# Nivona CafeRomatica NICR 695 research ledger

## Identity lock

- Canonical route: `/espresso-machine/nivona-caferomatica-6xx/`
- Permanent ID: `CD-EM-000213`
- Scored product: current titanium Nivona CafeRomatica NICR 695, EU/EEA 220-240 V generation introduced alongside NICR 690 in 2022.
- Current Nivona Netherlands material presents NICR 690 as the black color sibling and NICR 695 as titanium. German material listed only the 695 when checked.
- Older NICR 660, 670, 675 and 680 machines are excluded from the score. They use a different milk layout and older connectivity claims.
- This is not a North American review. No 120 V, North American warranty or North American availability claim is made.

## Claims ledger

- Current manufacturer page: three Aroma Balance profiles, five strength levels, three temperatures, five My Coffee slots, manual Easy or Mano SoloPlus Spumatore, two-cup espresso and coffee, hot water, 14 cm outlet, removable brew unit, bean sensor, 2.4-inch display, rotary control, 250 g hopper, 2.2 L water tank and 15-bar static pump rating.
- Current manual: 220-240 V, 1455 W, 2.2 L tank, 250 g beans, approximately 7-11 waste portions, approximately 9 kg and about 24 × 34 × 46 cm. Milk needs a valve operation and dedicated rinse and cleaning routines.
- Current German manufacturer price: 699 euros for NICR 695 on September 24, 2026.
- Current official product page dimension: approximately 24 × 34 × 48 cm. This conflicts with the current manual's 46 cm depth and is disclosed rather than averaged.
- Computer Bild tested exact-generation NICR 690, the black color sibling, and reported a balanced, full espresso, quiet grinder, straightforward controls and easy cleaning. These results were not reproduced by Coffeedant.
- Dmitry Yurchenko tested exact NICR 695 in 2022 and recorded 62 C milk, 66 C cappuccino and a 42-second first-cup readiness under his conditions. Those measurements are attributed and not generalized.
- The same independent review documents the key generation change: the 690/695 frother moved into the central outlet, so the cup stays put while the user still opens and closes the milk valve.
- Current owner material is self-selected and cannot support a defect rate, lifespan or repair probability.

## Model and region conflicts

- The family route name says 6xx, but the review identity must remain the exact current NICR 695.
- NICR 690 and NICR 695 are treated as color siblings only where current regional Nivona material lists them together. Delivered packages and regional listings still need confirmation.
- Bluetooth and app references for older 660-680 models are not copied to the current 690/695 generation. The current product pages and manual do not document app control.
- The official manual and product page conflict on 46 versus 48 cm depth. Buyers are told to allow at least 48 cm plus service clearance.
- German and Dutch regional availability differ. The German manufacturer page displayed the 695 only; the Dutch page displayed 690 and 695.
