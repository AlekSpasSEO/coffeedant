# Best Pour-Over Coffee Beans by Flavor Direction — source ledger

Checked October 29, 2026, Europe/Skopje. Scope: US whole-bean listings. This is a research-led selection by documented cone-filter role and flavor direction. Coffeedant did not buy, brew, taste or measure these bags. No delivered USD price, discount, stock guarantee or caffeine claim is made.

## Exact picks and evidence

1. **Counter Culture Apollo** — https://counterculturecoffee.com/products/apollo. Current official page calls the year-round organic blend medium roast and describes it as citrus, floral and silky. It lists 12 oz, 24 oz and 5 lb selectors. The checked page does not establish one Apollo-specific cone recipe, so the guide does not attribute a dose or target time to it. The linked full review contains additional bounded third-party and owner context. Its selection role is a floral/citrus first bag; skip if the buyer wants a chocolate-first or fixed-origin coffee.
2. **Onyx Geometry** — https://onyxcoffeelab.com/products/geometry and https://onyxcoffeelab.com/pages/help-me-brew. Current official page names washed Ethiopian and Colombian components, identifies both filter and espresso roles, and publishes an Origami/Hario cone start of 25 g coffee, 400 g water at 205°F. The general Onyx help page displays a different Geometry Origami guide; the guide uses the product-page recipe and warns that guide versions change. The linked review discusses recipe changes and owner context. Its selection role is rounded fruit with a specific cone starting point; skip for a dark roast or immutable single-origin lot.
3. **Blue Bottle Bella Donovan** — https://bluebottlecoffee.com/us/eng/product/bella-donovan. Current official US listing identifies a dark-roast Moka-Java-style blend with a natural Ethiopian component and attributed raspberry, chocolate and molasses notes. Its detailed pour-over guide calls for 27 g coffee and 350 g water, with 195–205°F water and the cooler end for darker roast. The linked full review handles subscription and freshness limits. Its selection role is a darker pour-over that can take milk; skip for a light, tea-like black cup.

General storage context: https://www.stumptowncoffee.com/blogs/news/coffee-class-get-fresh. This is not a product-specific shelf-life measurement. Full Coffeedant reviews linked from each pick contain independent/owner context with lot and method limits. Do not turn those sources into a comparative sensory ranking.
