# Quick Mill Alexia Evo 0970-A-CEVO research ledger

Research checked September 24, 2026. The published review is research-led; Coffeedant did not test, taste with, measure, photograph, service or disassemble this machine.

## Identity and lifecycle

- Scored identity: Quick Mill Alexia Evo, North American product ID 0970-A-CEVO, 110 V.
- The checked base model is a reservoir-fed E61 single boiler with PID and shot timer. It is not a heat exchanger.
- Flow control is not documented as included with the checked base SKU. Dealer-installed and owner-installed E61 needle-valve kits remain separate configurations.
- Excluded identities: the older Alexia, Eliane, regional 230 V versions and machines with undocumented modifications.
- Current status: Alexia was absent from Quick Mill's current home lineup on September 24, 2026. Espresso Outlet showed $1,550 and sold out; 1st in Coffee showed $1,550 and out of stock. Coffeedant did not find an official discontinuation notice, so current production remains unconfirmed.

## Specification and safety record

- One 0.75 L insulated boiler serves brewing, steam and hot water at different temperature ranges.
- The 58 mm E61 thermosiphon group uses a manual lever and three-way brew circuit.
- PID controls the boiler-temperature setting and provides a shot timer. It does not measure puck temperature or beverage yield.
- Water comes from a 3 L removable reservoir. There is no documented fixed-water connection for the scored configuration.
- Pump record: 52 W Ulka vibration pump with Quick Mill's Pulsar dampener.
- North American electrical record: 110 V, 1,400 W and 15 A. The rating plate must be checked on every unit.
- Specialist dimensions differ slightly by measuring method: about 9 to 9.5 inches wide, 17 to 17.5 inches deep and 15.25 to 15.875 inches high. Weight is about 38 lb.
- Initial boiler fill must follow the manual before heating. Hot E61 metal, steam, hot water and residual group pressure require normal espresso-machine precautions.

## Dated price and package

- Espresso Outlet and 1st in Coffee each displayed $1,550 on September 24, 2026, but neither showed available stock.
- Espresso Outlet described a one-year seller warranty. 1st in Coffee described a two-year seller warranty. Those terms are seller records, not a universal regional warranty.
- Retailer records identify single- and double-spout portafilters, blind basket, brew baskets, tamper and cleaning brush, but a remaining or used unit must be checked item by item.
- A used price needs adjustments for water history, scale, pump and element condition, E61 service, PID operation, accessories, modification quality, freight, tax and available technical support.

## Independent, owner and community evidence

- Kanen Coffee, LLC. published the exact-model video `Quickmill Alexia Evo Making A Shot Service Complete` on December 23, 2017. It shows one serviced machine completing a shot, not a controlled performance test.
- Foodal and the Home-Barista buyer guide cover older Alexia lineage. Their generation limits are preserved rather than silently transferred to 0970-A-CEVO.
- Home-Barista discussions repeatedly raise PID offsets, E61 group stabilization, grinder pairing, single-boiler milk sequencing and the inspection of older examples.
- One repair thread follows restricted flow after about six years of ownership. It is a diagnostic prompt for scale, pump, valves, jets and the group, not a model-wide failure rate.
- A Reddit discussion about an $800 example from 2018 illustrates used-market questions about age, water and service. It does not establish a current fair price.

No owner thread supplies a controlled denominator for failure incidence or expected service life. Voltage, water, grinder, roast, warm-up, service history and modifications differ.

This community evidence supplies bounded fit and inspection questions only. Unknown evidence includes failure incidence, remaining service life, measured warm-up, measured puck temperature, measured flow, measured pressure, measured noise and the condition of any unit Coffeedant has not inspected; those points remain unknown.

## Claims deliberately not made

- No Coffeedant access, tasting, temperature, pressure, flow, noise, steam, warm-up or extraction-yield measurement.
- No claim that PID boiler temperature equals puck temperature.
- No universal warm-up time, steaming time, flow-control setting, reliability rate or lifespan.
- No claim that the Alexia Evo is a heat exchanger or can brew and steam simultaneously.
- No claim that flow-control hardware is included, factory-installed or consistent across seller packages.
- No claim that absence from Quick Mill's current home lineup proves a formal discontinuation date.
- No claim that a sold-out $1,550 listing is a current transaction price.
- No reliability conclusion from one service video, one owner report or one repair thread.
