## Identity and region

This review covers the current North American Gaggia Anima listing with pannarello wand, black ABS front and 110–120 V specification. RI8760 suffixes sold elsewhere can change voltage, finish, wattage, warranty and accessories. It is not the Anima Deluxe Cappuccinatore or the Prestige carafe model.

## Current market evidence

Gaggia North America listed the machine at $699 on the check date. This is a dated new-stock US reference, not a global price or availability promise.

## Evidence decisions

- Official Gaggia pages and manuals control specifications, safety, grinder adjustment and care.
- Coffee Blog’s multi-week review and Whole Latte Love’s November 6, 2015 video provide independent workflow context.
- Owner discussions inform recurring questions about grinder settling, rinse water and brew-group care, not failure rates.
- Five grinder positions, three temperatures, five strengths and manual milk are described as controls, not as lab-measured performance.
