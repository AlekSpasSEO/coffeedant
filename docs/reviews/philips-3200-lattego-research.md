## Identity, lifecycle and price treatment

- Philips's US support record identifies EP3241/54 and marks it discontinued. Documentation, troubleshooting and parts routes remain online.
- The review does not assign one normal new price. Remaining, open-box, refurbished and used offers are condition-specific.
- Seattle Coffee Gear listed a B-class refurbished 3200 LatteGo at $379 with a 90-day retailer warranty on the check date. This is a dated offer benchmark, not a universal street price or a Coffeedant inspection.
- EP3241/54 is a super-automatic bean-to-cup machine with five labeled coffees: espresso, coffee, Americano, cappuccino and latte macchiato, plus hot water.
- Related 3200 suffixes can use a classic frother or add iced coffee. Their voltage, interface, drink list, finish and bundle are not transferred to EP3241/54.

## Disagreements and boundaries

- Reviewers broadly praise LatteGo cleaning but differ on espresso and long-coffee strength. The review presents the limitation as category-specific and gives controlled adjustment guidance.
- Milk may be foam-forward and less suitable for latte art than manual steam. “Tubeless” does not mean self-cleaning.
- AquaClean can delay descaling only when prepared, activated and replaced correctly under the manual's conditions. The review does not repeat an unqualified thousands-of-cups claim.
- Pump pressure is not used as a proxy for extraction quality.
