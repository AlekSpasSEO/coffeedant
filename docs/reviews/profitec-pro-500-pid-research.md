# Profitec Pro 500 PID current US exclusive research ledger

Research checked September 24, 2026. The published review is research-led; Coffeedant did not test, measure, photograph or disassemble this machine.

## Identity and official evidence

- Scored identity: Whole Latte Love SKU 25439, barcode 4260347830029, the current US-exclusive Pro 500 PID with a front display, automatic shot timer and black dual gauges.
- Excluded identity: older global Pro 500 PID revisions with a controller hidden behind the drip tray.
- The current Profitec product index no longer lists Pro 500. Whole Latte Love explicitly calls its current version an exclusive.
- The available manual is dated January 2018 and documents the older hidden-controller revision. It is used only for unchanged platform dimensions, electrical data, reservoir, water and safety guidance.
- The current base configuration does not include flow control. Retailer-installed flow control, wood accents and color selections are separate configurations.

## Specification and workflow record

- 2.0 L insulated stainless heat-exchanger boiler, 1,400 W element, E61 group, vibration pump, dual pressure gauges and simultaneous brewing and steaming.
- The PID controls steam-boiler temperature, which indirectly influences brew temperature through the heat exchanger and thermosyphon. It is not an independent brew-boiler controller or a puck-temperature sensor.
- Current-exclusive controls add the front PID, automatic shot timer, programmable ECO interval and backflush reminder.
- The older manual records a 2.8 L reservoir, 302 × 447 × 393 mm body without portafilter, 302 × 555 × 393 mm loaded depth, 23.5 kg, 115 V, 60 Hz and 1,400 W.
- Whole Latte Love’s current package lists a modular bottomless portafilter, silicone double-spout attachment, 7 g, 14 g and 21 g baskets, blind disc, 58 mm tamper, group brush and manual.

## Dated US availability

- Whole Latte Love: stainless, no-accent, no-flow-control configuration displayed at $1,999 with three-year parts-and-labor coverage.
- The listing was a preorder expected to ship by October 9, 2026 when checked.
- Clive Coffee displayed $1,999 but marked the machine sold out and described the older hidden-PID revision.

All price, stock and offer states above were checked September 24, 2026. They can change and do not establish a normal long-term market price.

## Independent and community evidence

- Home-Barista technical measurements from October 23, 2019 show how setpoint, idling and flushing interact on an older Pro 500 PID. Those measurements cannot be assigned to the current exclusive.
- Home-Barista owner reports discuss build, vibration-pump noise, steam, recovery and heat-exchanger routine on older machines.
- Reddit buyer discussions compare the Pro 500 PID with compact dual boilers and question whether milk volume justifies the heat-exchanger trade-off.
- Anthony’s Espresso Equipment uploaded a unique exact-model front-display video on March 16, 2024. It is a commercial dealer presentation, not independent laboratory evidence.

This community evidence supplies bounded owner questions only. Unknown evidence includes current-revision failure incidence, lifespan, measured group temperature, measured noise, measured steam performance and the condition of any unit not inspected by Coffeedant.
