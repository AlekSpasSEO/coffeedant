# Philips 2200 LatteGo EP2230/14 research ledger

Checked September 24, 2026. The reviewed product is the US 120 V Philips Series 2200 LatteGo EP2230/14 in matte black. This identity excludes the manual-wand EP2220/14, regional EP2230/10 and later 2300, 3200, 4300 and 5400 generations.

## Identity and official documentation

- Philips US exact product page names EP2230/14, three fresh-bean beverages, LatteGo, matte black and touch controls.
- Philips US exact support remains active and links current documentation, parts and troubleshooting. No explicit discontinuation statement was found, so the review does not call the model discontinued.
- The current Philips/Versuni family manual covers 800, 1200, 2200 and 3200 variants. It supports operating and care procedures but does not erase regional or trim differences.
- Encompass maintains an exact EP2230/14 parts and diagram record. Its page calls the machine a 3200 Series in one label, so the exact model number, not that family label, controls.

## Confirmed product facts

- Direct fresh-bean recipes: espresso, coffee and cappuccino. Hot water is available but is not counted as a fourth coffee beverage.
- Two-piece, tube-free LatteGo milk container.
- Twelve grinder positions, three aroma-strength positions, three drink-volume positions and three temperature choices.
- Bean and preground inputs, two-cup black-coffee selection, removable brew group and AquaClean support.
- Family documentation and exact listings converge on a 1.8 L water tank, approximately 275 g bean hopper and body around 24.6 × 37.1 × 43.3 cm W×H×D.
- AquaClean claims are conditional on correct activation and timely filter replacement. No unconditional no-descaling claim is made.

- Coffeeness tested EP2220/14 with the manual steam wand. Its control, grinder and basic platform observations are attributed as adjacent-family context only; its milk and cup findings are not assigned to EP2230/14.
- MRM Reviews published an independent EP2230/10 LatteGo tutorial review on January 18, 2024. The video shows a closely related regional machine, not the US voltage, bundle, price or warranty.
- Best Buy's 68 exact-model reviews provide self-selected owner context. Ease of use and coffee quality recur, while tank-size and ordinary container-management comments appear. Ratings are not reliability data.
