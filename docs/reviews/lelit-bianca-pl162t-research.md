- A representative failure rate, average repair cost, and expected lifespan are unknown.
- Finish-specific inventory, included accessories, and promotions can change after the check date.
- Exact warmup and temperature behavior depend on settings, electrical region, room conditions, and measurement method.
- Paddle and programmed low-flow benefits depend on coffee and operator skill; no universal profile is supported.
