## Maintenance boundary

- Follow prompts for circuit rinsing, cleaning tablets, filters and descaling. Automatic hydraulic draining can place water in the tray.
- Empty and wash the tray and grounds collector. Wipe, purge and clear the steam nozzle as directed after milk.
- The brew group is not documented as owner-removable. Do not open the appliance for internal cleaning or repair.
