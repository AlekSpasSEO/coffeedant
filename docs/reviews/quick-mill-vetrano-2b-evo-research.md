## Product facts accepted

- Insulated 0.75 L brew and 1.4 L steam boilers, listed as stainless steel by both current US sellers.
- 58 mm E61 group, manual brew lever, PID temperature control with shot timer and dual brew plus steam manometer.
- Vertically mounted rotary pump, 3 L bottom-fed reservoir, selectable direct-water inlet and drainable drip tray.
- Current packages list plumb and drain kits, single- and double-spout portafilters, baskets, 58 mm tamper, and two- plus four-hole steam tips.
- 110 V operation with configurable 15 or 20 A logic. In 15 A mode the controller alternates boiler-element demand; 20 A allows both elements together.
- About 12 to 13 W × 18 D × 16 H inches, 5.375-inch cup clearance and 70 lb.
- Independent coffee- and steam-boiler switches plus separate 60-minute steam and 120-minute full eco actions.
- No automatic shot stop, standard flow-control device, integrated grinder, app or brew-by-weight claim was accepted.

## Conflict and limitation record

- Current seller width differs: Chris Coffee lists 13 inches, Whole Latte Love lists 12 inches. The review keeps the range and asks buyers to measure.
- Current seller prices differ by US$100 and may include different setup, support or promotional terms.
- The manual and seller context establish 15 or 20 A logic, but Coffeedant did not measure recovery in either mode.
- Flow control and joystick valves appear in dealer and review records as options, not standard scored equipment.
- Unknown evidence includes representative brew temperature, full-group warm-up, recovery, steam rate, sound level, pump-pressure consistency, long-term failure incidence, average service cost and parts turnaround.
- Owner praise, one defective example, a stiff lever, basket fit and one slow-fill repair thread do not establish average reliability.
- Plumbing requires a water-quality, pressure, shutoff, leak-protection and drain plan. No universal water recipe was invented.
