# Fellow Espresso Series 1 — research ledger

- Canonical route: `/espresso-machine/fellow-espresso-series-1/`; permanent ID `CD-EM-000247`.
- Checked October 14, 2026, Europe/Skopje. Scope is the current US black Espresso Series 1 and its checked accessory package. Regional voltage, other finishes and future hardware or firmware revisions are not conflated.
- Method: research-led editorial assessment. Coffeedant did not own, operate, taste from, photograph, measure, repair or durability-test the unit. The six scores are supported judgments about a buying decision, not bench or sensory results.
- Evidence classes: **official** Fellow product, support, price, material, care and warranty pages; **independent** WIRED, Tom’s Guide, Good Housekeeping and James Hoffmann’s video; **community** selected owner posts; **unknown** production failure rate, future software support, real delivered cup performance and repair costs.
