## Product facts accepted

- Separate seller-listed 0.75 L brew and 1 L steam boilers, both insulated stainless steel in current US descriptions.
- Independent boiler switches allow espresso-only use or simultaneous brew and steam.
- PID temperature control with shot timer, E61 brew lever and dual manometer.
- 3 L top-fill reservoir beneath a hinged cup tray, with low-water alarm.
- Vibration pump and reservoir-only feed. No current stock direct-plumb or rotary-pump claim.
- 11.25 W × 17.75 D × 16.25 H inches, 5.75-inch cup clearance and 50 lb.
- 110 V, 15 A current US listing. Seller technical tables disagree on precise heater split.
- Current packages list single- and double-spout portafilters, baskets, blind disk, 58 mm tamper, and two- and four-hole steam tips.

- Unknown or unresolved evidence includes representative brew temperature, warm-up, recovery, steam rate, sound level, pump-pressure consistency, long-term failure incidence, average service cost and parts turnaround. Coffeedant made no measurements and found no controlled current-SKU laboratory evaluation that resolves those points.

- Some historical pages describe copper boilers. Current US sellers describe stainless boilers; the current SKU boundary controls.
- Owner warm-up times are routines, not Coffeedant measurements. Boiler-ready and fully heated E61 states are distinct.
- Flow control is discussed as an option, not stock equipment.
- Positive multi-year ownership and individual repair threads do not establish average service life or failure rates.
