## Identity and generation boundary

The current official EU page names `LPSCCS01EU`. Its official product sheet was generated September 23, 2026. The current manual family covers `LPSCCS01` and matte-black `LPSCCB01` along with Evoluzione variants. Older `CCC`, `LPSCCC01`, V2 and generic Cellini pages remain online and can show different dimensions, gauges, pressure controls or regional hardware.

Cellini Evoluzione and Cellini Evo PID are not the reviewed machine. Evoluzione can add a rotary pump and direct-water capability, while the Classic remains a vibration-pump, tank-fed, pressurestat HX. Claims from those variants were not transferred.

## Exact official facts adopted

- Copper 1.8 L single boiler with heat exchanger.
- Brass, chromed E61 brewing group and 58 mm internal filter-holder diameter.
- Vibration pump with a 15-bar pump rating. This is not presented as pressure at a coffee puck.
- Mater pressurestat, boiler manometer, automatic boiler-water level and anti-vacuum valve.
- 2.9 L removable tank, tank-empty alarm and no direct water connection.
- Separate stainless cool-touch steam and hot-water wands with rotary Bakelite knobs.
- Passive cup warmer, removable stainless tray and steel cup grid.
- 295 × 430 × 370 mm, 23 kg net, 1.8 m cord.
- Single- and double-spout portafilters, 7 g and 16 g filters, blind filter, ABS tamper, scoop, brush and filter gasket.

## Editorial conclusion

The current Cellini Classic is best understood as a traditional 58 mm E61 HX platform. Its decision case is simultaneous manual espresso and steam, conventional service hardware and current exact documentation. Its trade-offs are heat-soak and flush discipline, vibration-pump noise, tank-only water, no PID or shot timer, and the additional cost of grinder, scale, water treatment and specialist support.
