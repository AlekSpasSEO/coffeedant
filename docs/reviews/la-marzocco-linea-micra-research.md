## Disagreements and unknowns

- Manufacturer dimensions conflict on the same page. Independent measurement aligns with the technical table.
- Cup clearance and the short wand bother some reviewers and not owners using bottomless mode, thin scales and small cups.
- The polymer convertible portafilter is thermally purposeful but feels less premium to some owners.
- Five-minute readiness is independently supported for a normal shot; Kaffeemacher also found a limit in a compressed professional-style queue.
- No installed-base evidence proves a ten-year lifespan or failure rate. Owner issue threads are diagnostic, not statistical.
- App behavior and accessory compatibility can change after publication.
