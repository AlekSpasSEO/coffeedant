# Melitta Passione OT F53/1-102 research ledger

Checked September 24, 2026. This ledger supports `/espresso-machine/melitta-passione/` and permanent product ID `CD-EM-000210`. The assessment is research-led. Coffeedant did not operate, taste from, measure, open or durability-test this machine.

## Identity and region

The reviewed product is the black Passione OT F53/1-102 pictured in the [current Melitta international gallery](https://www.melitta-international.com/bean-to-cup-machines/our-categories/all-bean-to-cup-machines/Passione-OT) as article 6767348. The same page pictures the silver F53/1-101 as article 6767344, while its shared technical table labels model F531-101. The review therefore treats black and silver as closely related colour suffixes but keeps article, plug, voltage, accessories, warranty and rating plate explicit. It does not merge the milk-free Passione, Latticia OT, CI Touch or Barista TS Smart.

## Current official specification

Melitta lists 10 preset variations, four direct classics, five coffee strengths, 85/90/95 °C temperature settings, five grinder steps, 25–220 ml water adjustment, pre-brewing, a single 125 g hopper, 1.2 L water tank, 1450 W, 220–240 V, 8.3 kg and 390 × 253 × 380 mm H×W×D. The outlet is described as height-adjustable to 135 mm.

The current table explicitly lists no app, My Coffee profiles, programmable reference keys, two-chamber hopper or ground-coffee shaft. Coffee Double Cup is supported, while two simultaneous coffee-milk specialities are explicitly not supported.

## Milk and care

The official record lists cappuccino, latte macchiato, warm milk and milk foam through a hose-fed one-touch system. Easy Steam Cleaning rinses milk-bearing paths with hot water and steam. Melitta also says the milk parts are removable, the supplied container is dishwasher-safe and the brew group can be removed for physical cleaning. The [operating manual](https://www.melitta.de/media/0c/e0/00/1701262008/Passione_manual_total_Web.pdf) remains the authority for the delivered machine.

## Independent and community context

[Dinside](https://dinside.dagbladet.no/bolig/dette-er-de-beste-espresso-maskinene/69388475) reported on January 30, 2018 that Passione OT led a seven-machine Testfakta comparison. The result is useful older comparative context, not a current exact-black evaluation. [Vinbarista](https://vinbarista.com/en/blog/melitta-caffeo-passione-one-touch-coffee-machine-review-thread866.html) published a May 26, 2017 overview of the footprint, display, outlet and milk workflow without a disclosed controlled method.

Current owner evidence is self-selected. [Galaxus](https://www.galaxus.at/en/s2/product/ratings/melitta-caffeo-passione-ot-automatic-coffee-makers-9795210) snippets include praise for coffee and simple operation plus milk-system concerns. A [Reddit comparison thread](https://www.reddit.com/r/superautomatic/comments/1hv6t27/melitta_vs_nivona_help/) contains one general care-dependent reliability view. A [Komtra technical thread](https://komtra.de/forum/index.php/Thread/17875-Melitta-Passione-OT-F53-1-101-Caffeo-Kaffeebezug-bricht-bei-starkem-Kaffee-mit-P/) documents one F53/1-101 with a high-strength brew interruption after about 3,080 drinks. None establishes a representative failure rate.
