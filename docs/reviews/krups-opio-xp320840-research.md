- Krups UK identifies article XP320840 in black, 220–240 V and 50 Hz.
- The exact product page and model-indexed support route link the XP320840 instructions.

## Maintenance boundary

- The exact manufacturer guidance calls for brew-head, holder and filter cleaning after use, tray emptying and washing, immediate steam-nozzle wiping and purging, and manual descaling as directed.
- The exact table does not identify dishwasher-safe parts.
- Stop use and seek qualified service for leaks, electrical smells, repeated protection-device trips or abnormal heat.

## Editorial conclusion

The XP320840 is a basic manual espresso platform with traceable UK documentation but sparse independent measurement. Its value remains unresolved until an exact unit is actually available from a trustworthy seller with clear coverage. A complete comparison must include a grinder and accessories rather than the machine price alone.
