## Official evidence

ECM's current Classika PID page and operating instructions identify a 0.75 L stainless single boiler, E61-style group, PID with shot counter, low-noise vibration pump, about 2.8 L tank, Fast Heat-Up at roughly 11 minutes, ECO mode, cleaning reminder, 250 × 445 × 395 mm dimensions, and 18.5 kg mass. ECM documents the E61 Flow Profile Valve separately as an accessory. The review therefore never assumes flow control is installed, included, calibrated, or covered by a seller's standard warranty.

## Independent evidence

Whole Latte Love and Clive Coffee provide current retail, control, warmup, espresso, steam, and package context. The observed US price is $1,649 before any assumed flow-control cost. Whole Latte Love's video is older than the latest fast-heat specification, so current facts come from ECM while the video is limited to visible machine and workflow context.

## Community evidence

The cited r/espresso threads discuss Classika operating routines, pump pressure, PID interpretation, flow-control comparisons, single-boiler milk limits, and grinder pairing. Many owner machines include dealer-installed or owner-added accessories, and temperature-measurement methods vary. Community reports guide setup questions but do not establish factory performance or reliability.

## Unknown or unresolved

Long-term current-revision incidence, real readiness under every voltage and room condition, group-to-display offset on an individual unit, optional-valve calibration, measured flow, steam rate, temperature recovery, sound, and repair cost remain unknown. Seller bundles and prices can change. Coffeedant has no physical test, taste result, Scace measurement, or teardown.

## Editorial decisions

The review treats Classika as an espresso-first machine. PID, timer, E61 handling, and optional flow control earn strong espresso and feature scores. One boiler makes milk a secondary task. Puristika is the espresso-only alternative, Silvia Pro X the dual-boiler milk answer, and Profitec GO the more direct compact PID route.
