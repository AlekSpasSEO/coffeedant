## Identity, lifecycle and live offer

- Reviewed product: Elizabeth PL92T, specifically PL92T-120 for the North American 120 V / 60 Hz Type B configuration.
- Current offers: Lelit US and Clive Coffee both showed $1,799.95 and add-to-cart controls.
- Lelit North America warranty: 12 months, non-transferable, for eligible authorized consumer purchases used within region. Clive separately advertised three-year parts-and-labor coverage for its eligible sale.
- Successor: Elizabeth3 is PL92T3, a separate machine. Its official page lists 120 V codes and Pagaia hardware, but no reliable US launch date or price was confirmed.
- Category: compact reservoir-fed dual-boiler semi-automatic with timed coffee buttons, LCC control and vibration pump.

## Disagreements and unknowns

- The LCC can show readiness earlier, while Kaffeemacher and CoffeeKev recommend about 20 minutes for stronger thermal equilibrium.
- Lelit’s official 32 × 38 × 38 cm body and independent “main case” measures differ because projections and axes are handled differently.
- Reviewers praise internal capability while disagreeing about thin panels, sharp edges, plastic controls and included tools.
- Timed dose buttons are sometimes casually called volumetric. Official language and Clive’s current specifications support programmed brewing time, not a flow meter.
- PL92T3 US price, launch date, mature firmware behavior and independent long-term record were unknown.
- Owner repair anecdotes do not establish a failure rate for either generation.
