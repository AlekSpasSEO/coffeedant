# Espresso, drip and rapid cold coffee in one US machine

Checked 2026-11-12, Europe/Skopje. Research-led editorial guide; Coffeedant did not operate, time or taste from these machines. No Amazon API or offer data used. No current price, discount or orderability claim.

## Decision and originality

The existing `/espresso-machine/built-in-grinder/` guide compares whether to integrate the grinder and includes portafilter and superautomatic machines. This article answers a different requirement: a single **portafilter** machine with maker-documented espresso, ordinary drip coffee and rapid cold coffee. The current exact US Premier ES601 and Pro ES701 both meet it. The buyer then decides whether lever tamp, independent hot water and the larger milk package justify Pro. The separate `/espresso-machine/best-espresso-machines-for-back-to-back-milk-drinks/` article addresses concurrent brew and steam, which is a different bottleneck.
