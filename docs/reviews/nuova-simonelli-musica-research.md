# Nuova Simonelli Musica research ledger

## Identity lock

- Canonical route: `/espresso-machine/nuova-simonelli-musica/`
- Permanent ID: `CD-EM-000215`

- Direct-connect/plumbed Musica and Lux/LED trim are excluded from the exact product score.

## Claims ledger

- Current Nuova Simonelli family page and US records: 2 L copper heat-exchanger boiler, thermoregulated group, electronic pre-infusion, simultaneous brewing and steaming, programmable volumetric doses and 58 mm portafilter.
- Current US installation record: 110-125 V, 1,200 W, 10 A and NEMA 5-15, with an approximately 13 × 17 × 16-inch countertop envelope.
- Current manufacturer dimensions: 32 × 43 × 40 cm, about 20 kg, stainless steel plus ABS.
- Exact controls: programmed single and double doses, continuous/manual brewing, dedicated hot water, boiler gauge and adjustable lighting.
- No user-facing brew-temperature PID, integrated shot timer, grinder, scale or app is documented for this exact machine.
- Current exact-version price: $2,950 at Seattle Coffee Gear on September 24, 2026, available to add to cart with a vendor-shipping notice.
- Independent exact-model evaluations consistently identify steam power and simultaneous milk workflow as the principal advantage. Coffeedant did not reproduce those observations.

## Conflicts and limits

- Reservoir capacity conflicts across official records. The official brochure says 3.0 L while the US manual states 2.3 L. The review discloses both and asks the buyer to confirm the delivered tank.
- Musica is sold in reservoir and direct-connect configurations and in standard Black and Lux/LED trims. A generic Musica listing does not prove the exact scored configuration.
- Current Pro Coffee Gear pricing supports market context but does not clearly lock the displayed unit to the same reservoir-only configuration.
- A boiler-pressure gauge does not report water temperature at the puck.
- Volumetric stopping repeats measured water movement, not beverage mass after puck absorption.
- Manufacturer parts documentation supports exact part identification and qualified service planning. It does not prove a failure rate, repair cost or component lifespan.
