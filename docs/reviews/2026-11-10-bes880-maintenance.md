# Barista Touch BES880 US maintenance clarification

Checked 2026-11-10 08:57 Europe/Skopje (+01:00). Canonical review: `/espresso-machine/breville-barista-touch-bes880bss/`, permanent ID CD-EM-000122. Research-led; no Coffeedant physical test or price claim.

Source: [Breville BES880 USCM instruction book](https://assets.breville.com/BES880/BES880_USCM_IB_AB26_LR.pdf), English pages 23–24. The Settings menu defines Clean Cycle as cleaning the shower screen and back-flushing the group head; Care & Cleaning explicitly says the prompted cleaning cycle is separate from descaling. The manual directs a Descale Cycle for mineral buildup, and separately describes brushing the removable upper burr and grinder chute to remove coffee-oil buildup. [Breville US BES880 product page](https://www.breville.com/en-us/product/bes880) confirms current US model identity and integrated grinder.
