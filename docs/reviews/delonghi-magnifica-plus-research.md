## Editorial decisions

The review rewards the combination of 18 recipes, four profiles, full-touch control, and automatic hot milk because those features reduce repeated work in a shared home. It marks the absence of true cold extraction and direct espresso control clearly. Magnifica Evo Next is the value alternative, Dinamica Plus the premium interface step, and Eletta Explore the hot-and-cold route.
