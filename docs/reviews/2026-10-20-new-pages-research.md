## Machine and grinder evidence boundaries

Profitec lists a 1.6 L stainless heat-exchanger boiler, 2.8 L tank, three-position boiler-temperature switch, selectable preinfusion, shot display, bottomless modular portafilter and 7/14/21 g baskets for JUMP. The [current manual](https://www.profitec-espresso.com/media/pages/produkte/jump/1566e8deef-1778593535/ba_pro400-jump.pdf) is shared with Pro 400; JUMP has a distinct item number and should not inherit Pro 400 results. [Kaffeemacher’s exact-model video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l_kZgJboDa4) was published August 31, 2024. [Owner report one](https://www.reddit.com/r/espresso/comments/1qkok9c/review_help_my_experience_with_the_new_profitec/) and [owner report two](https://www.reddit.com/r/espresso/comments/1tq0k05/disappointed_in_the_profitec_jump/) are self-selected, conflicting steam/build observations, not a reliability rate. The review does not equate boiler switch position to measured brew temperature at the coffee.

Eureka specifies 65 mm hardened-steel flat blind burrs, a revolution-counting stepless dial, 5° tilt, 45 g hopper and bellows. It claims about 0.55 g total retention and dose-output consistency under its factory method. [Kaffeemacher’s independent Zero comparison](https://kiffeemacher.com/en/blogs/kaffeewissen/eureka-zero-vergleichstest) and [compendium](https://kaffeemacher.ch/en/blogs/kaffeewissen/eureka-mills) report 1.8 g absolute retention for their sample/protocol. These are different quantities and cannot be averaged. [Clive’s exact-model instructions](https://support.clivecoffee.com/en/articles/16426660-eureka-mignon-zero-65-ap-espresso-grinder-user-manual) describe the cup, feed tab and startup. [DieRöster’s exact-model video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ctsbwb5RaAY) was published December 23, 2024; it is separate from Kaffeemacher’s measurements. Owner accounts are self-selected. The regular/decaf listicle recommends dedicated equipment for a strict separation need and otherwise describes cleaning/purging without claiming a measured caffeine dose.
