## Decision and identity

Buying question: Is the Plus premium worthwhile for a household that wants quick home espresso and milk drinks, while still grinding, dosing and tamping manually?

Verdict hypothesis: automatic milk makes the Plus attractive for frequent latte/cappuccino drinkers; espresso-only buyers should test whether the cheaper Bambino leaves more budget for a capable grinder. Buyers wanting adjustable brew temperature or bean-to-cup automation have different requirements.

## Disagreements and publication limits

* Plus and base model must stay separate throughout title, comparison, owner anecdotes and commerce links.
* Do not retain simultaneous brew-and-steam prose; Serious Eats primary hands-on review explicitly confirms these cannot happen together.
* Old claims that US Plus includes only pressurized baskets conflict with current US manual and must be removed.
* Three-second readiness is a heating-system claim. It does not mean a complete latte in three seconds or a preheated portafilter/cup.
* 15-bar pump rating is not the stated brewing pressure. Use Breville's stated 9-bar extraction, not a Coffeedant measurement.
* Exact dimensions remain unresolved. Do not normalize source disagreement by averaging or reversing axes without proof.
* No verified current ASIN, Amazon price, star count, inventory, warranty duration, service fee or failure rate.
* No scored Coffeedant test, first-hand taste notes, ownership duration, or original measurement exists.
* An 18 g dose is within manual's 16–19 g double-basket range. A proposed 36 g yield may be framed as a suggested dial-in starting point; it is not the 60 ml volumetric factory preset or a guaranteed optimum.
