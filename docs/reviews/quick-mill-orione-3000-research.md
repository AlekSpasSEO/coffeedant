# Quick Mill Orione 03000 research ledger

Research checked September 24, 2026. The published review is research-led; Coffeedant did not test, taste with, measure, photograph, service or disassemble this machine.

## Identity and lifecycle

- Scored identity: current Quick Mill Orione 03000, EAN 8007062300033, European 230 V configuration.
- Quick Mill currently names the product Orione. The legacy Coffeedant route and retailer records retain Orione 3000 or 03000.
- Current manufacturer evidence establishes a thermostat-controlled single-thermoblock machine with no PID.
- Excluded identities: historical 3000 revisions, 120 V machines, regional packages, seller-only feature claims and owner-installed PID or pressure modifications.
- Current status: Quick Mill publishes Orione in its current range. Crema showed €759 including VAT, zero stock and unconfirmed availability on September 24, 2026.

## Specification and safety record

- One die-cast aluminum thermoblock contains a copper heating coil longer than 0.5 meter and serves coffee and steam sequentially.
- Quick Mill specifies separate coffee and steam thermostats plus a safety thermal fuse. The current page explicitly says PID is absent.
- The brew group uses Quick Mill's O-ring seal. Crema lists a 58 mm portafilter for product number 13288.
- The manual steam wand includes an aerator. No simultaneous brew-and-steam function is documented.
- The official side-access tank is 1.8 L. Fixed plumbing is not documented.
- Quick Mill lists a 0-16 bar gauge, vibration pump, lever switches, power light and heating light.
- Official dimensions are 247 × 330 × 375 mm and weight is 8.8 kg. Retailer dimensions and weights conflict, so official figures govern.
- Official power is 1080 W with 200-240 V and 120 V variants. The scored unit is the European 230 V configuration.
- The rating plate, plug and delivered manual must govern safe electrical use. Hot water, steam, hot metal and mains electricity require normal espresso-machine precautions.

## Dated price and package

- Crema displayed €759 including VAT, zero stock and unconfirmed availability on September 24, 2026.
- Crema also showed a €739 outlet card. Outlet condition, package and availability were not treated as normal new-stock evidence.
- NaturBohne and Coffeeness provide additional market context but publish conflicting or differently dated specifications and price records.
- Tax, delivery, voltage, plug, warranty, baskets and accessories differ by seller and region.
- A used price needs adjustment for age, voltage, thermostat behavior, water history, scale, leaks, pump and thermoblock condition, baskets, modifications, service and local parts support.

## Independent, owner and community evidence

- Kaffeemacher independently purchased and tested an Orione, then followed the machine after extended flat-share use. The record covers temperature routine, espresso, steam, basket fit and service context.
- The unique video `Quick Mill Orione 3000 - Hat sie 2 Jahre WG-Leben überlebt?` was published by Kaffeemacher on March 18, 2023. It is independent long-term context, not Coffeedant testing or a reliability-rate study.
- Coffeeness independently reviewed the machine and frames it as a compact manual choice for one or two people, with quick water heating and limited milk throughput.
- Kaffee-Netz owner threads discuss thermal preparation, blank-flush routines, grinder sensitivity, straight-espresso fit, cleaning, parts and long use. These are self-selected experiences across multiple revisions.
- One recent Reddit owner describes an added PID and OPV. That configuration is deliberately separated from stock equipment.

No owner thread supplies a controlled denominator for failure incidence or expected service life. Voltage, thermostat, shower plate, water, grinder, roast, warm-up, service history and modifications differ.

This community evidence supplies bounded fit, workflow and inspection questions only. Unknown evidence includes failure incidence, remaining service life, measured warm-up for every revision, measured puck temperature, measured flow, measured pressure, measured noise and the condition of any unit Coffeedant has not inspected; those points remain unknown.

## Claims deliberately not made

- No Coffeedant access, tasting, temperature, pressure, flow, noise, warm-up, steam-time or extraction-yield measurement.
- No universal heat-up time, flush routine, temperature, pump-pressure setting, reliability rate or lifespan.
- No claim that the front gauge measures flow or beverage yield.
- No claim that current Orione has PID, programmable pre-infusion, a shot timer, volumetric dosing, direct plumbing or simultaneous brew and steam.
- No transfer of an owner-installed PID or OPV to the stock product.
- No silent resolution of conflicting retailer tank, dimension, weight, power or feature records.
- No reliability conclusion from one long-term test, one ten-year owner or one modified machine.
