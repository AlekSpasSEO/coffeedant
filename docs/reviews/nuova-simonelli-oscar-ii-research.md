# Nuova Simonelli Oscar II research ledger

## Identity lock

- Canonical route: `/espresso-machine/nuova-simonelli-oscar-ii/`
- Permanent ID: `CD-EM-000216`
- Scored product: current US black Oscar II reservoir/pour-over version, 110-125 V and NEMA 5-15.
- Direct-connect Oscar II, red and blue regional variants, and 220-240 V machines are excluded from the exact product score.
- Oscar Mood is a separate current product family and does not lend its controls or features to Oscar II.
- The current global page shortens the name to Oscar, while its brochure and current US archive identify the same dimensions and platform as Oscar II. The US archive still lists Oscar II among active machines before its discontinued section.

## Claims ledger

- Current official product and US records: 2 L copper heat-exchanger boiler, thermocompensated group, pre-infusion, simultaneous brewing and steaming, 58 mm filter holder and four-hole articulated steam wand.
- Current US installation record: 110-125 V, 1,200 W, 10 A and NEMA 5-15, with a 12 × 16 × 16-inch countertop envelope.
- Current manufacturer dimensions: 300 × 400 × 408 mm W×H×D, 13 kg, stainless steel plus ABS.
- Exact controls: two time-programmable coffee buttons plus manual stopping and a push-pull steam lever.
- Official brochure records a 3 L reservoir and approximately 1 L drip tray. There is no dedicated hot-water outlet or plumbed tray drain.
- No user-facing brew-temperature PID, pressure gauge, integrated shot timer display, grinder, scale, app or flowmeter-based volumetric dosing is documented for this exact machine.
- Current exact-version price: $1,995 at WebstaurantStore on September 24, 2026 for item 238OSC2POBL, black, pour-over and 110 V.
- Independent exact-model evaluations consistently identify steam power and compact simultaneous milk workflow as the principal advantage. Coffeedant did not reproduce those observations.

## Conflicts and limits

- The global manufacturer page says Oscar while the current US official archive and linked brochure say Oscar II. Dimensions, platform, controls and linked records align; the review discloses the naming difference instead of treating Oscar Mood as the successor identity.
- Seller pages sometimes call timed coffee buttons volumetric. The official programming guide shows that the buttons store pump-on time, not flowmeter-measured water volume.
- The reservoir and direct-connect versions have different installation needs. The direct-connect option does not establish that the scored pour-over machine can be converted with a hose.
- Prima demonstrated a direct-connect unit and reports no plumbable drain. Its throughput and steam-timing statements are attributed observations, not Coffeedant measurements.
- Manufacturer parts and electrical documentation support exact part identification and qualified service planning. They do not prove a failure rate, repair cost or component lifespan.
- Owner discussion about pressure modifications mixes years, regions and altered machines. It is not transferred to the current exact configuration without serial-level evidence.
