## Identity and official evidence

This review covers the second-generation North American Z10 Aluminium Black NAB, article 15702. Earlier Z10 reviews are not treated as exact-generation specification sources. Current JURA material and the NAB manual support 51 Hot, Light, Cold and Sweet specialties, Cold Extraction, four profiles, P.R.G.2+ grinder, Coffee Timer, Caffeine Regulator, Milk Assistant, Quality Assistant, a 4.3-inch touchscreen, included Wi-Fi, HP3 milk system, 81 oz water tank, 9.9 oz hopper, 12.6 by 14.3 by 18.5 inch body and approximately 27 lb weight.

The official US shop showed $4,499 and out-of-stock status on September 23, 2026. That is dated price and availability evidence, not a worldwide price or stock guarantee. The optional chocolate attachment is not represented as standard equipment.

## Independent, community and unknown evidence

T3's February 13, 2026 launch coverage supplies current independent price and feature context. An ECS Coffee cold-brew video is clearly labeled as first-generation workflow evidence only. A September 2025 owner discussion about the new generation and a February 2026 negative owner report are both retained as community context. They identify questions about value, menu breadth, grinder settings, software and expectations but do not establish a defect rate.

Unknown evidence includes Coffeedant-measured extraction yield, cold-drink temperature, caffeine content, milk texture, noise, energy use, reliability and service life. The review does not turn JURA feature names into laboratory claims. It also does not assume that 51 menu entries equal 51 materially distinct extraction methods.
