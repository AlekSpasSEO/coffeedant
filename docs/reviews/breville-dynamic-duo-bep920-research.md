## Official evidence

Breville US defines BEP920 as the BES920 Dual Boiler paired with the BCG820 Smart Grinder Pro. The machine supplies a 58 mm group, independent PID brew and steam boilers, simultaneous use, programmable preinfusion, and front-filling convenience. The grinder supplies 60 settings and timed Dosing iQ. The official bundle price was $1,699.95 on September 17, 2026. Contents and finish pairing remain region sensitive.

## Independent evidence

Seattle Coffee Gear's November 21, 2020 Crew Review shows the two-appliance footprint, grinding, 58 mm preparation, extraction, and steam workflow. It is a retailer-produced independent demonstration, not Coffeedant testing. Separate official component pages are used to prevent broad bundle language from hiding the different machine and grinder capabilities.

## Community evidence

A separate Smart Grinder Pro discussion covers adjustment and upgrade paths. These community records are self-selected, are not necessarily exact BEP920-bundle ownership, and are not instructions for unapproved modification.

## Editorial decisions

The BES920 drives the high espresso, milk, and feature scores. The BCG820 is treated as a credible starting grinder and the likely first performance ceiling. Value is therefore conditional: a real bundle discount is strong, while a small discount favors buying the machine alone with a deliberately chosen grinder.
