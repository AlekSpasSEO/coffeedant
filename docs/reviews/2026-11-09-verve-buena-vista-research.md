[Verve's current US product page](https://www.vervecoffee.com/products/buena-vista-dark-roast), checked November 9, identifies the exact Buena Vista Dark Roast as a Southern Colombian blend, dark roast 8/10, with official descriptors chocolate, red apple and maple; its body copy describes dark chocolate and caramelized sweetness. Variety and processing are not specified for the whole blend. It displayed 6 oz, 12 oz, 2.2 lb and 5 lb selectors, and a $22 12 oz one-time setting before tax and delivery. Verve says roast to order with shipping within two business days; verify the received roast date. The page offers espresso (18.7–19 g in, 33–36 g out, 23–27 s), Dwell (20 g/300 g, 2:15) and French press (35 g/525 g, 4:30) starts. They are roaster starts, not Coffeedant measured results.

[Emily Preston's 2016 home review](https://rustyfoxfarm.com/2016/12/08/verve-coffee-roasters-buena-vista-dark-roast/) preferred a French press to her Chemex with an older Buena Vista bag, while saying she did not clearly perceive the claimed dark-chocolate note. This is an attributable independent personal sample, but the old roast, earlier notes and unknown current component mix do not establish a November 2026 bag result. Do not transfer her old price. [One r/espresso owner](https://www.reddit.com/r/espresso/comments/1ot0d6o/verve_beans_breville_bambino_plus_baratza_encore/) reported difficult sour or thin extractions on a Bambino Plus and Encore ESP in 2026; this is setup-specific, not a defect rate or representative outcome. The roaster's espresso recipe supplies a more appropriate starting point than extrapolating that owner result.

Editorial fit: a US buyer seeking the roaster's darker chocolate/caramelized-sweetness direction for espresso, press or milk. First pack 12 oz or 6 oz if available; verify roast date, selected whole-bean format and delivered total.
