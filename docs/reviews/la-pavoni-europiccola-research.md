## Performance and safety boundary

No controlled exact-current-model dataset was found for brew temperature, lever force, puck pressure, shot repeatability, full thermal stability, steam time, sound or energy use. The official 0.8-bar value is boiler pressure and is never represented as pressure at the puck. Independent accounts commonly allow roughly 10 to 15 minutes before brewing, but that is practical context rather than a factory readiness guarantee.

The boiler, group, wand and other exposed metal become hot. The direct-fill boiler must not be opened while pressurised. The page follows the manual boundary: switch off, release residual pressure and cool before removing the cap or refilling. It does not endorse defeating the thermostat, pressurestat or safety valve.

## Editorial conclusion

The current Europiccola Lusso is a compact, complete heated-lever platform rather than an easy pump-machine substitute. Its decision case is tactile pressure control, integrated steam, simple metal construction and current support. Its trade-offs are exposed heat, pressure-release safety, direct-fill refilling, thermal sensitivity, slow group service and the separate cost of grinder, scale, tamper, pitcher, water and learning time.
