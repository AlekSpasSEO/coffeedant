## Official specification record

- CoffeeSelect: three bean containers, 150 g, 210 g and 180 g; 540 g total. The product sheet lists one conical grinder, not three grinders.
- Water container 2.2 L, glass milk container 0.7 L, drip tray 0.8 L, waste container 16 servings.
- CM Touch control panel, 10 programmable profiles, Expert mode, heated cup storage, CupSensor and BrilliantLight.
- Programmable ground quantity, water, temperature, milk, froth and pre-brewing; selectable grind and ground-coffee chute.
- Single and paired coffee and milk recipes, coffee pot, teapot, tea temperatures, hot water, hot milk and froth.
- AutoDescale cartridge, automatic milk-pipe rinse, ComfortClean, removable milk pipework and removable brew unit.
- 311 × 420 × 445 mm W×H×D, 80–160 mm spout range, 1.45 kW, 120 V, 15 A.
- Included: glass milk container, descaling cartridge and cleaning tablets.
- The manual identifies touch-display operation and schedules physical cleaning for the milk flask and path, tray, waste bin, water tank, CupSensor, spout, bean system, ground-coffee chute and brew unit.
- Miele warns that additional manual descaling can be necessary depending on use.

## Independent evaluation

- Coffeeness publishes an exact-model hands-on review by Arne Preuss, updated January 7, 2025 and fact-checked by Nicholas Marshall.
- It praises espresso, milk foam, grinder noise, build and interface while emphasizing the unusually high price.
- It reports 10 profiles, 2.2 L water, one conical grinder, three containers totaling 540 g, a pre-ground bypass, 16-serving waste capacity, 1,450 W and 14.2 kg.
- Sensory language and usability judgments remain attributed. Coffeedant did not reproduce them.

## Editorial scoring rationale

- Espresso 8.7/10: meaningful automatic recipe controls and positive attributed hands-on context, limited by one shared grinder and fixed automatic brew path.
- Milk 9.0/10: broad single and paired recipes with programmable milk and froth, balanced by real cleaning work.
- Workflow 8.8/10: three beans, 10 profiles, CupSensor and 16-serving capacity are unusually useful, but the cabinet and menu system are substantial.
- Build 8.3/10: removable-brewer access and substantial construction are positives without proving lifespan.
- Features 9.5/10: CoffeeSelect, AutoDescale, CupSensor, CM Touch, profiles, pots and connectivity are exceptional; no cold-extraction suite is documented.
- Value 6.1/10: the $4,499 dated price requires frequent use of the uncommon multi-bean and multi-user workflow.
