- Method: research-led; Coffeedant did not physically test this machine

## Identity and region

The review covers the Stone Black EG2115/01 sold for 230 V markets. Gaggia's official page documents a factory PID, stainless-steel boiler, 15-bar pump, automatic pre-infusion, 1.2 L side tank, plastic group, ABS and stainless-steel filter holder, two supplied double-wall filters, ESE pod support, classic pannarello and 19.9 by 30.3 by 25.5 cm cabinet. It is not a Gaggia Classic, and the evidence does not support calling it a 58 mm platform.

## Current market evidence

Gaggia Direct UK showed £199 including VAT, out of stock, with a five-year parts-warranty statement on the check date. That is a dated regional listing rather than permanent availability. The product page gives 230 V and 1900 W, so the review does not imply North American compatibility.

## Evidence decisions

- Official Gaggia product and product-search records control the exact features, electrical package and supplied accessories.
- Whole Latte Love's March 25, 2024 video provides independent visible workflow context; Coffee Blog supplies an additional independent evaluation.
- Reddit owner discussion is community evidence used to identify clearance, accessory and modification questions, not to establish extraction measurements or reliability rates.
- A community claim that the heater is a thermoblock conflicts with Gaggia's “stainless steel boiler” wording. The review records the official wording and leaves unverified internal geometry unknown.
- The legacy page's first-person test, extraction-yield, temperature-stability and fixed pressure claims were unsupported and were removed.
