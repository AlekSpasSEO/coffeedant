## Official evidence

Breville US and the current BES881 manual anchor the 54 mm portafilter, integrated grinder, intelligent dose guidance, assisted tamp lever, ThermoJet heating, touchscreen drink sequence, and Auto MilQ system. The checked base-finish price was $1,499.95; premium finishes reached $1,699.95. This is an assisted semi-automatic, not a one-touch superautomatic, because the owner transfers and cleans the portafilter.

Reviewer observations are used with method and commercial caveats. They do not become Coffeedant extraction, temperature, or timing results.

## Editorial decisions

Guidance, dosing feedback, tamp assistance, and Auto MilQ drive high workflow and feature scores. The 54 mm platform, sequential heater, high price, and integration of grinder, screen, tamp mechanism, and milk in one body constrain build and value. Finish surcharges are separated from coffee function.
