## Identity boundary

The review scores the current Domobar Digital documented on VBM's live product page, family brochure and June 2024 data sheet. It excludes Domobar Junior Digital, Domobar Super heat-exchanger machines, unidentified older revisions, modified machines and used examples without an exact rating-plate and manual match.

The current factory record calls this a single boiler with a 0.5 L boiler, vibration pump and OLED interface. Retailer copy describing a TFT touchscreen, heat exchanger, two heating circuits or pressure profiles conflicts with VBM and is not used for product architecture.

## Product facts accepted

VBM documents a 128 × 64 OLED with capacitive keyboard, one 0.5 L boiler, 40 mm pressure gauge, vibration pump, removable sensor tank, one steam wand, drawer drain, 1,400 W electrical load and a 22 × 40 × 37 cm, 15 kg body. The VBM 1961 thermosiphonic chromed-brass group uses a manual lever, mechanical preinfusion and 58 mm tools.

The interface supports coffee temperature, steam temperature, brew count-up, low-tank warning, inactivity and timeout alerts, temperature-unit choice and standby. The product page also documents a drip-tray float, dishwasher-safe synthetic tray and magnetic side panels.
