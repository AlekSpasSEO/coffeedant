## Identity and region

This review covers current North American RI8702/46 at 110-120 V. Gaggia North America documents thirteen specialties, including the current Over-Ice coffee and espresso programs, an integrated removable milk carafe, 2.4-inch color TFT, five-step ceramic grinder, five aroma strengths, removable brew group and 60.9 oz water capacity. The global 230 V page lists twelve beverages, so its menu count and electrical specification are not silently merged with the North American product.

## Current market evidence

Gaggia North America listed both black and Moonlight Grey finishes at $899 on the check date. Tax, stock, bundles and warranty can change. The Over-Ice program is represented as a programmed hot extraction intended for ice, not cold brew.

## Evidence decisions

- Official Gaggia North America and global documentation control model identity, drink menu, capacities, care and regional differences.
- Whole Latte Love's May 3, 2021 model review and Coffeeness's written review provide independent workflow and comparison context.
- Owner discussions supply community evidence about recipe order, milk amounts, grinder settling, cleaning and the lack of named profiles. They do not establish defect rates.
- The legacy page's fixed drink temperatures and unsupported “premium-tier” performance claims were removed. Exact in-cup temperature and long-term reliability remain unknown without a documented Coffeedant protocol.
