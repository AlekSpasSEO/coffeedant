## Identity, generation, and live offer

- Reviewed product: current United States 120 V Ascaso Steel Duo PID V3, cross-checked against Ascaso's current global 2026 Steel specification sheet.
- Official US offer at check: black machine listed at $2,095 on the Ascaso USA product page.
- Current US electrical specification: 2000 W, NEMA 5-20 plug, with a 15 A adapter included. The intended 20 A installation is part of the buying decision, not a minor accessory note.
- Heating classification: two thermoblocks, one for brewing and one for steam. The machine can brew and steam simultaneously, but it is not a dual boiler.
- Canonical model boundary: Steel Duo PID is distinct from Steel Duo, Steel Duo Plus, Steel Uno PID, older V1 and V2 units, and regional voltage versions.
- Current official US product copy lists a 12-month general warranty and a five-year headline for thermoblocks and groups. The separate terms, exclusions, seller eligibility, shipping, labor, and remedy control the practical coverage.
