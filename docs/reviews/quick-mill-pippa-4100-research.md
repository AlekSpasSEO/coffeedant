# Quick Mill Pippa 4100 research ledger

Research checked September 24, 2026. The published review is research-led; Coffeedant did not test, taste with, measure, photograph, service or disassemble this machine.

## Identity and lifecycle

- Scored identity: current Quick Mill Pippa platform in the North American 120 V configuration commonly sold as Pippa 4100; Whole Latte Love SKU 29515 is the dated US market anchor.
- Quick Mill currently names the product Pippa. The legacy Coffeedant route and market records retain the 4100 designation.
- Current manufacturer evidence establishes a thermostat-controlled 0.45 L brass single boiler with no PID.
- Excluded identities: historical revisions, other voltages, retailer-exclusive finishes and packages, seller-only claims and owner-adjusted or modified machines.
- Current status: Quick Mill publishes Pippa in its current range. Whole Latte Love showed US$999, reduced from US$1,095, but sold out on September 24, 2026.

## Specification and safety record

- One 0.45 L insulated brass boiler serves coffee and steam sequentially.
- Quick Mill specifies coffee and steam thermostats plus a safety reset thermostat. PID is explicitly absent.
- The ring brew group is paired with a retailer-listed 58 mm commercial-style portafilter.
- Quick Mill describes an anti-burn multidirectional steam and hot-water wand. No simultaneous brew-and-steam function is documented.
- The official side-access tank is 1.8 L. Fixed plumbing is not documented.
- Quick Mill lists a vibration pump with claimed 30% noise reduction, lever switches, indicator lights and external expansion-valve access.
- Official dimensions are 246 × 336 × 375 mm and weight is 14 kg. North American retailer dimensions and weights vary.
- The current official global page lists 1280 W and both 120 V and 200–240 V variants; North American sellers commonly list 1000 W. The rating plate and delivered manual govern.
- Official copy describes a 0–3 bar boiler gauge while North American sellers describe a 16-bar brew gauge. The review preserves that unresolved regional conflict.

## Dated price and package

- Whole Latte Love displayed US$999, reduced from US$1,095, and marked the machine sold out on September 24, 2026.
- A Canadian seller displayed C$899 sale pricing for a black unit against C$1,098 regular pricing on the same date.
- Returned, demo and bundle prices are not treated as ordinary new-stock references.
- Tax, delivery, voltage, plug, warranty, finish, baskets and accessories differ by seller and region.
- A used price needs adjustment for age, voltage, boiler priming history, water, scale, leaks, thermostat cycling, pump, gauge, valve adjustment, baskets and service.

## Independent, owner and community evidence

- Homegrounds independently reviewed Pippa and covers the single-boiler workflow, 58 mm tools, PID and pre-infusion omissions, pressure-valve access, heat-up and comparisons.
- The unique video `Quick Mill Pippa Review: Best Entry-Level Italian Espresso Machine?` was published by iDrinkCoffeeCanada on January 28, 2025. It covers the current product, espresso, steam and buyer fit.
- Artisti demonstrates unboxing, espresso preparation and milk texturing. It is a retailer-led evaluation; taste and package claims remain attributed.
- Reddit owner threads discuss finish, grinder pairing, warm-up, espresso results, steam sequence and comparative value.
- A Home-Barista thermostat repair thread supplies a bounded service example only.
- An owner pressure-valve video shows access, not a safe universal setting.

No owner thread supplies a controlled denominator for failure incidence or expected service life. Voltage, finish, gauge, water, grinder, roast, warm-up, service history and adjustments differ.

This community evidence supplies bounded fit, workflow and inspection questions only. Unknown evidence includes failure incidence, remaining service life, measured warm-up for every revision, measured puck temperature, measured flow, measured pressure, measured noise and the condition of any unit Coffeedant has not inspected; those points remain unknown.

## Claims deliberately not made

- No Coffeedant access, tasting, temperature, pressure, flow, noise, warm-up, steam-time or extraction-yield measurement.
- No universal heat-up time, thermostat-surf routine, pressure setting, reliability rate or lifespan.
- No claim that the front gauge has the same range or purpose in every region.
- No claim that Pippa has PID, programmable pre-infusion, a shot timer, volumetric dosing, direct plumbing or simultaneous brew and steam.
- No transfer of an owner adjustment or repair to the stock product.
- No silent resolution of conflicting manufacturer and retailer power, gauge, dimension or weight records.
