1. [Rocket Appartamento TCA product page](https://rocket-espresso.com/products/domestic-models/appartamento-tca) establishes the current heat-exchanger identity, 1.8 L boiler, vibration pump, tank sensor, dimensions, power, and product-page reservoir figure.
2. [Rocket TCA brochure](https://cdn.sanity.io/files/zhlpxy7s/production/f6be58bcd76781b4c2bd99be8a9cb112402d0553.pdf) describes four boiler-pressure presets and the redesigned group.
3. [Rocket Appartamento and TCA manual](https://links.imagerelay.com/cdn/2615/ql/8bec8d8781e54b59800b05a669fdb864/Rocket-espresso-appartamento-tca-user-manual.pdf) governs the 115 V setup, 1.8 L boiler, 1.9 L tank, 1200 W element, programming, cleaning, and fault guidance.
4. [Rocket dealer route](https://rocket-espresso.com/dealers) states that warranty responsibility runs through authorized dealers in the country of purchase. Exact duration, labor, and freight terms must be obtained from the chosen seller.
5. Whole Latte Love's current TCA parts diagram supports assembly identification and service planning.

## Independent evidence

Espresso Outlet, Coffeeness, Men's Journal, Coffee Blog, and Coffee Friend supply hands-on, comparison, and daily-use context. They help explain the four settings, heat-exchanger idle state, steaming, counter presence, and original-versus-TCA distinction. Where a review repeats the brochure's capacity figures rather than the current manual, the disagreement stays visible instead of being averaged away.

Selected video: [Whole Latte Love, Rocket Appartamento TCA Espresso Machine Review](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=duStXQ2aoKM), published January 26, 2024. This is a commercial demonstration of the exact TCA generation. Manufacturer documents remain the authority for regional specifications and safety.

## Community evidence

Three separate r/espresso pools were used: a critical TCA review, a mixed buyer-remorse/value discussion, and a new-owner learning-curve thread. They reveal concerns about temperature interpretation, grinder and puck preparation, flush routines, and value. They cannot show prevalence, defect rate, or the experience of a representative owner.

## Unknowns and disagreements

- The current page and manual say 1.9 L reservoir and 1200 W, while the official brochure says 2.5 L and 1350 W. The delivered regional rating plate is decisive.
- The four pressure zones influence both brewing and steam. They are not independent brew-boiler temperature set points.
- Retailer degree charts are orientation, not guaranteed puck-temperature measurements.
- Population-level failure rate, expected life, local labor price, parts lead time, and warranty-transfer rules remain unknown.
- Flow-control kit compatibility and warranty consequences require current dealer or manufacturer confirmation.
