## Official product and operating boundary

The [Profitec JUMP product record](https://www.profitec-espresso.com/en/products/jump) lists a 1.6 L stainless heat-exchanger boiler, 2.8 L reservoir, vibration pump, 228 × 448 × 372 mm body without portafilter, 21.5 kg weight, three-position boiler-temperature switch, PID management, selectable preinfusion, eco mode, shot display, low-water alert, dual gauge and a modular portafilter with bottomless option and 7/14/21 g baskets. The [current combined Pro 400/JUMP manual](https://www.profitec-espresso.com/media/pages/produkte/jump/1566e8deef-1778593535/ba_pro400-jump.pdf) must be read at model-specific instructions; sharing the PDF does not make the machines identical. The switch selects boiler presets, not a directly measured coffee-bed temperature. [Clive’s setup](https://support.clivecoffee.com/en/articles/16427127-profitec-jump-setup-guide) and [user manual](https://support.clivecoffee.com/en/articles/16427128-profitec-jump-user-manual) clarify first fill and preinfusion. Their dealer role is disclosed.

The [Whole Latte Love US listing](https://www.wholelattelove.com/products/profitec-jump-espresso-machine) displayed $2,059 for American Walnut without flow control on October 20. Other handles and optional flow control are separate selections; tax, shipping, stock and future offers may change. No expiry or completed checkout was verified. This base machine requires a separate grinder, scale and routine care. Do not call a machine price a complete station cost.

## Independent and commercial context

[Kaffeemacher’s JUMP versus Pro 400 video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l_kZgJboDa4), published August 31, 2024, demonstrates and compares its own exact-model sample. It is independent context for heat-exchanger controls, not Coffeedant extraction or a universal thermal specification. [Soave’s JUMP evaluation](https://soave.ca/learning-centre/profitec-jump-review/) discusses a Canadian dealer sample and is explicitly commercial. Whole Latte Love’s ability-to-make-several-milk-drinks statement is also a seller judgment. Neither proves steam speed or texture on every US unit. A dual boiler comparison such as the existing Profitec Pro 600 review addresses buyers who need more direct separate brew and steam control.
