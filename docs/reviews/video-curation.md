# Product video reviews

`src/data/product-videos.json` is the shared editorial catalog for `/videos/` and every equipment/bean review. The first selection is the single review-page embed; the library shows up to three per product. Existing legacy `review.video` fields are research seeds only and are never automatically published.

For every new product review, search YouTube for the exact brand and model (including generation, suffix, milk system, or coffee blend). Rank candidates for useful demonstrations, critical evaluation and clear model relevance. Prefer complementary creators. A retailer is third party but must be labelled `sourceType: retailer`; manufacturer, subsidiary and official brand-channel uploads are excluded. Do not substitute another generation or non-decaf blend to fill a slot. Never pad to three with poor or irrelevant footage.

Check the public watch page for the original title, channel name and channel URL, full publication timestamp, duration, public availability and permission to embed. Write an original short summary of what the source actually covers. Record affiliate links or supplied-equipment disclosures where applicable. No fabricated transcripts, dates, audience counts or testing claims. Do not identify Coffeedant as the video creator. Keep the original language rather than assuming every upload is English.

Add zero to three entries under the canonical review slug in `product-videos.json` and record the assessment date/status in `product-video-assessments.json`. An empty array plus `no-suitable-third-party-video` is the correct result when no suitable embeddable third-party source is available. Recheck older entries when refreshing a review; remove unavailable videos and re-rank replacements.

After building, run `node scripts/research-product-videos.mjs` to discover candidates for new products. `--refresh --limit=10` researches existing products, and `--pending --limit=10` resumes metadata verification for saved `pendingVideoIds`. Results are research files, not automatic selections. Stop on service limits and retry later; never fabricate missing metadata. `pending-metadata-verification` records a blocked lookup separately from a genuine no-video result. On October 16, 2026, YouTube limited further public metadata reads after the main catalog had been verified; those remaining candidate IDs are retained in the assessments for completion.

Run `CF_PAGES=1 npm run build`, then `npm run validate:videos`. The build inventory discovers current and future equipment and coffee reviews automatically. CI rejects an unassessed new product, orphaned selections, more than three videos, missing credits/dates/durations, duplicate entries, a review with more than one embed, and video/schema mismatches. Every selected video carries `VideoObject` metadata; the hub adds `CollectionPage`, `ItemList` and breadcrumbs. YouTube players are created on click, use the privacy-enhanced domain, and stop when filters change.

Brand/model filters include all video products. The existing Coffee Finder retains its espresso-machine catalog and applies the same preference logic to video groups; with no preferences selected it leaves grinders, brewers and coffee beans visible.
