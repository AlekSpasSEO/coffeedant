## Publication checks

- Captions transfer knowledge first, then attach creator, source, and license.
- Alt text describes what is visible and does not repeat the caption's full argument.
- Images do not imply Coffeedant used the pictured equipment or physically tested the Bambino Plus.
- No image is used solely to meet a density quota. Together with the product hero and independent video, the seven editorial images keep the long article visually paced at roughly one useful visual per 500 words.
- The CC BY-SA image is displayed without editorial cropping. If it is modified later, record the modification and confirm the resulting share-alike obligations.
