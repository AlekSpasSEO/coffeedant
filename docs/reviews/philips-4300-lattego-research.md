# Philips 4300 LatteGo EP4347/94 research ledger

Research checked September 24, 2026. The published review is research-led; Coffeedant did not test, measure, photograph or disassemble this machine.

## Identity and region

- Scored identity: US Philips 4300 LatteGo EP4347/94.
- Philips’ exact US support page explicitly marks EP4347/94 discontinued but still provides current documentation, troubleshooting, cleaning and parts access.
- Regional EP4346/70 and other 4300 codes are not treated as interchangeable.
- Philips’ archived US product page unexpectedly prints 230 V and 50 Hz. Seattle Coffee Gear’s exact US EP4347/94 record says 120 V, 1350 W and 10 A. The review discloses the conflict and directs buyers to verify the physical rating plate.

## Official specification record

- Eight fresh-bean coffee recipes: espresso, coffee, Americano, cappuccino, latte macchiato, café au lait, caffè crema and ristretto.
- Milk froth and hot water are additional outputs.
- Two user profiles plus Guest; five aroma strengths; three temperature settings; adjustable coffee and milk length.
- 12-position ceramic grinder, preground bypass, 275 g hopper, 1.8 L front-access tank, 0.26 L LatteGo and 12-serving waste container.
- 246 × 372 × 433 mm and about 8 kg.
- Double-cup coffee service is supported; milk double-cup service is not.
- Removable brew group, guided descaling, AquaClean compatibility and dishwasher-safe LatteGo and tray parts.

## Dated US availability

- Seattle Coffee Gear exact certified-refurbished EP4347/94: $579, three units shown, six-month retailer warranty.
- Walmart Business restored EP4347/94: $529, sold and shipped by Seattle Coffee Gear, 30-day returns.
- Philips official EP4347/94R0 open-box record: $999, out of stock, six-month warranty.
- Philips current official US range displayed the newer 2300 at $549.99. This is successor context, not an EP4347/94 price.

All prices and stock states above were checked September 24, 2026. They can change and do not establish a normal long-term market price.

## Independent and owner evidence

- Coffeeness hands-on EP4347-family review, updated May 30, 2025, supplies attributed espresso, foam, interface and maintenance observations. Its settings and sample do not predict another refurbished unit.
- Seattle Coffee Gear’s exact-generation Crew Review was published April 30, 2021. It shows controls, LatteGo and workflow. The publisher also sells the product, so its commercial interest is disclosed.
- Exact-model retailer reviews and Reddit discussions are self-selected. Recurring themes include easy profiles and milk cleanup, plus disagreements about coffee strength, foam, rinses and faults. These are inspection questions, not incidence rates or lifespan evidence.

This community evidence supplies bounded owner questions only. Unknown evidence includes failure incidence, remaining service life, measured temperature, measured noise and the condition of any unit not inspected by Coffeedant; those points remain unknown.
