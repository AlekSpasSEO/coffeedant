## Official evidence

Breville US anchors the original Oracle generation as BES980 or BES980XL by regional SKU. It automates grinding, dosing, and tamping into a removable 58 mm portafilter, uses independent PID brew and steam boilers, and automates milk temperature and texture. It uses physical controls and a text display rather than the later touchscreen interfaces. The checked official price was $2,199.95.

## Editorial decisions

The dual boilers, automated 58 mm preparation, and simultaneous milk justify strong espresso and milk scores. Build and value are constrained by dense integration, older controls, and a checked price $200 above the Oracle Jet base finish. The article keeps BES980 separate from BES990 Oracle Touch and BES985 Oracle Jet.
