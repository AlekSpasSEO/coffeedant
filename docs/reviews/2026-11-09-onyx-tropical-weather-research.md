## Identity and claims

[Onyx Tropical Weather](https://onyxcoffeelab.com/products/tropical-weather), checked November 9 Europe/Skopje, is a light-roast Ethiopian blend of washed and natural microlots. Onyx calls it evergreen while explaining that the actual lots and ratio can rotate. The page lists mixed variety, filter and espresso use, and roaster flavor words mixed berries, sweet tea, raw honey and plum. Its story's 50/50 phrasing is not treated as a fixed future-bag formula. The storefront displayed 2 oz, 10 oz, 2 lb and 5 lb selectors, but redirected the browser to an international URL; the US delivered checkout total was not verified. The recipe visible on the current page is a filter start of 15 g coffee to 250 g water at 200°F, with a 3:00 drain target. It must not be presented as an espresso ratio.

[Onyx brew guidance](https://onyxcoffeelab.com/pages/help-me-brew), checked November 9, establishes product-specific method context. [Onyx FAQ](https://onyxcoffeelab.com/pages/faq) discusses its 10 oz box as a container and larger-bag closure; none of that proves the delivered roast date. [Counter Culture freshness guidance](https://counterculturecoffee.com/blogs/counter-culture-coffee/coffee-basics-freshness) is general storage context only.

## Buyer judgment

A smaller first bag is appropriate for a buyer deliberately seeking the roaster's fruit-and-tea direction across filter and espresso; separate recipes are required. A roast-forward milk drink or fixed-origin requirement points to a different coffee. Check the actual roast date, whole-bean size, stock and US delivered total before purchase. These are editorial inferences from the named records, not sensory measurements.
