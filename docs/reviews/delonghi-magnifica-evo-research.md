## Disagreements and unknowns

- “Magnifica Evo” covers automatic-carafe and manual-frother packages. Review and commerce claims are restricted to ECAM29084SB.
- Seven icons do not represent seven unrelated brewing engines; several drinks combine shared coffee, water and milk systems.
- Reviewers differ on default strength, temperature and milk texture. The article gives adjustment logic without promising a taste or cup temperature.
- Grinder changes need several cycles to become evident. No Coffeedant retained-ground measurement exists.
- Automatic operation still requires refilling, emptying rinse water and pucks, carafe care, brew-group rinsing and descaling.
- Owner complaints and long-use reports do not justify a failure rate or expected lifespan.
- Retailer packages, accessories, availability and price can differ from the exact direct offer.
