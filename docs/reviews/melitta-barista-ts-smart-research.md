## Cleaning and ownership

Melitta describes guided coffee-circuit cleaning and descaling, a removable brewer, removable milk system, dishwasher-compatible supplied milk container and Easy Steam Cleaning with hot water and steam. Those functions reduce prompts and rinsing effort but do not sanitize every detached hose, connector or food-contact surface without physical cleaning.

The international page says a Pro Aqua filter can reduce descaling to once per year under a stated scenario: six 120 ml drinks daily and six timely filter changes. The current UK technical table says “once a year descaling: No.” The review preserves the condition instead of promising an annual interval to every owner. Actual water hardness, drinks, filters and prompts govern the schedule.

The [current UK FAQ](https://www.melitta.co.uk/faq/bean-to-cup-machines/barista-ts-smart) covers brew-unit reinstallation and settings troubleshooting. It supports the exact service path but not owner repair of internal electrical or hydraulic parts.

## Independent evaluation

[Bethan Morgan’s T3 review](https://www.t3.com/home-living/coffee-machines/melitta-barista-ts-smart), published April 13, 2025, reports a hands-on setup and drink trial. Morgan liked the screen, espresso, heat-up and thorough rinse routine, found café crème bitter in that setup, called the machine large, and described it as noisy and clunky. The article does not expose a current black article suffix, so its observations remain family-level context.

[Sarah Warwick’s Real Homes review](https://www.realhomes.com/reviews/melitta-barista-ts-smart-coffee-machine-review), published November 13, 2019, reports quick setup, easy drink selection, positive espresso and cappuccino impressions, two-cup use and eight profiles. Warwick described the drink temperature as ready-to-drink and hot enough. This is useful counter-context to owners who wanted hotter drinks, not a current-model lab measurement.

[Her Favourite Food](https://www.herfavfood.com/blog/melitta-ts-smart-coffee-machine-review) records two months of supplied-machine use in 2020, praising consistency, espresso and milk results. The disclosure and older context limit it to another independent perspective, not a reliability study.

No comparable independent dataset was located for dose, beverage temperature, extraction yield, sound, energy, milk temperature, clean-water consumption or component life. The review leaves those quantities unknown.
