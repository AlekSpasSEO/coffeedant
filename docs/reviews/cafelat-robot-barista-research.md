## Official evidence

Cafelat's UK product page and official Robot documentation anchor the Barista identity. The reviewed package has the installed pressure gauge, professional basket, bottomless portafilter, clip-on spout, screen, papers, mat, tamper, and current listed accessories. Cafelat describes stainless steel, aluminium, and silicone construction, roughly 3 kg mass, about 31 cm height with the arms down, and about 46 cm with the arms level. It has no heater, pump, grinder, or milk system.

## Independent evidence

Prima Coffee supplies specialist basket, chamber, and dose context. Alternative Brewing's February 17, 2020 video clearly shows the basket, water fill, two-arm press, gauge, cup clearance, and cleanup. It is retailer-produced visual evidence, not Coffeedant testing. The £383.99 direct price was checked on September 17, 2026.

## Community evidence

The Cafelat Robot community and r/espresso search records were used to identify recurring questions around preheating, puck preparation, pressure profiles, grinder choice, accessories, and low-noise daily use. These are self-selected reports across different coffees, grinders, and skill levels. They do not establish performance or reliability rates.

## Unknown or unresolved

Unknowns include market-wide seal and gauge failure incidence, long-term parts availability by region, exact duties and delivery outside the UK, and how much preheating a particular coffee and recipe will require. Coffeedant has no pressure, temperature, extraction, force, or taste measurements.

## Editorial decisions

The espresso and build scores reward direct control and simple mechanics. Milk scores low because no milk system exists, even though a separate device can complete a latte station. Value is assessed against the complete grinder, kettle, scale, and milk plan rather than the brewer alone.
