# No-plug manual espresso guide — November 5, 2026

Canonical: `/espresso-machine/best-manual-espresso-makers-no-plug/`. US market. Research-led buying guide. Coffeedant did not acquire, use, measure or taste output from the compared brewers. No current price, discount, inventory guarantee, measured extraction or reliability rate is claimed.

## Decision and route audit

This guide compares the way heat and pressure reach a manual espresso brewer when the brewer itself has no plug. The existing `/espresso-machine/small/` concerns counter footprint; `/espresso-machine/cheap-budget-under-500/` concerns a verified machine budget; `/espresso-machine/without-grinder/` concerns modular grinder choice. None is a no-plug heat-source decision. The public sitemap, current source routes, aliases and registry URL Map were checked. The two included full-review routes are established canonical pages, not new product reviews.

The current US Flair Classic product listing describes the revised kit with a pressure gauge, a Flow Control portafilter, Bottomless 2-in-1 portafilter, thin brewing cylinder and manually heated water. The US 9Barista Mk.2 listing requires a Standard/Pro and stove-type choice. This guide selects Standard and tells buyers to select the correct heat plate. The 9Barista brewer body stays the same across stove choices; the supplied plate changes. No Mk.1 or Pro-specific performance claim is applied to Standard. The brewer is not a normal moka pot.
