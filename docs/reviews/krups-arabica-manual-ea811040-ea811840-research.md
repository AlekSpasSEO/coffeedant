## Identity, official instructions and limitations

The [Krups UK black EA811040 page](https://www.krups.co.uk/ESPRESSO-MACHINES/BEAN-TO-CUP-MACHINES/Arabica-Manual-EA811040-Espresso-Bean-to-Cup-Coffee-Machine-Black/p/8010000003) and [silver EA811840 support page](https://www.krups.co.uk/instructions-for-use/Products-/Beverages/Full-automatic/KRUPS-Arabica-Manual-Bean-to-Cup-Coffee-Machine--Silver-EA811840-/csp/8000036153) establish distinct UK article numbers. The black page specifies physical strong/standard buttons, rotary volume, three grind positions and three temperature settings, 1.7 L water, 220–240 V, 1450 W and manual steam. Its marketing text says 250 g bean capacity while its specification table lists 260 g. Neither is resolved by inventing a measurement. The separately listed 1.8 L replacement-tank accessory is not proof of the machine's nominal capacity. The [2019 version-3 EA81 family manual](https://dam.groupeseb.com/m/62cdd0a0d9492811/original/8020003842-IFU.pdf?timestamp=20251212155752) is linked from the exact black model support page. It documents initial rinse, drink ranges, two sequential coffee cycles, grind adjustment only while grinding, manual steam with delayed shutoff, nozzle washing and tablet/descaling prompts. It describes a separately sold XS6000 milk accessory, not a bundled one-touch carafe. Match the actual rating plate and supplied manual before ordering parts.
