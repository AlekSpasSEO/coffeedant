# Krups Virtuoso XP442C40 research ledger

Checked September 24, 2026. This ledger covers the exact UK XP442C40 in stainless steel and black. It does not transfer plug, voltage, package, controls, performance or support from regional XP442C variants or the later Virtuoso+ XP444GG0.

## Exact identity and official documentation

- Krups UK identifies article XP442C40 with a UK plug, 220–240 V, 50–60 Hz and 1400 W.
- The exact product page and support route link manual revision 1820007472/02 for Pump Espresso Virtuoso XP442.
- The manufacturer documents a thermoblock, 15-bar pump rating, 1 L removable tank, removable tray, manual steam and hot-water outlet, cup warmer and spoon-tamper.
- Coffee options are one- and two-cup ground-coffee filters and a separate ESE soft-pod filter.
- The one-cup button can be programmed from 15 to 65 ml and the two-cup button from 30 to 130 ml. Defaults are 40 ml and 80 ml.
- The manual gives 280 × 143 × 285 mm, 1400 W, 220–240 V and domestic use only. Currys Business lists 3.5 kg and EAN 3016661166938.
- The current product table lists 0.2 W off mode and 28 minutes to off mode. The older manual says automatic shutdown after 30 minutes. The page records both rather than hiding the revision difference.
- Fifteen bar is a pump rating. No exact pressure at the coffee, brew temperature, warm-up protocol or independent repeatability data was found.

## Workflow and care

- The panel has power, steam, one-cup and two-cup buttons. A side knob selects preheat or steam and hot water.
- After steaming, the manual requires a water flush through the steam nozzle until the coffee circuit returns to its preset temperature.
- Krups recommends 60–100 ml cold milk, a short water purge before milk, about 10 seconds near the bottom before moving the jug, and no more than 45 seconds of continuous steam for 100 ml.
- Owners should clean the holder and filters after use, wipe the brew head, empty and wash the tray, and purge and wipe the nozzle immediately after milk.
- No appliance parts belong in a dishwasher. The automatic descaling program alternates pump and pause periods and must be followed by clean-water rinses.
- The manufacturer states that limescale-related repairs may not be covered by the two-year domestic guarantee.
