## Identity boundary

The scores and Product schema apply only to the retired North American 110 to 120 V Rocket Giotto Cronometro R with rotary pump. The exact historical listing and the Giotto and Mozzafiato Cronometro R manual define the scored product: a 1.8 L insulated copper heat-exchanger boiler, boiler PID, 58 mm chrome-plated brass E61 group, manual lever, rotary pump, reservoir or direct-water path, hidden shot timer, dual gauges, hot-water tap and articulating no-burn two-hole steam wand.

The review does not merge the vibration-pump Giotto Cronometro V, flat-sided Mozzafiato Cronometro R, older Giotto Evoluzione R, 220 to 240 V regional builds, modified machines or current Giotto FAST R. The current Giotto FAST is successor context only.

## Product facts accepted

- Historical North American exact-model record: 110 to 120 V, 1200 W and 10 A.
- Historical exact-model dimensions: 13.19 W × 16.53 D × 15.75 H inches and 50.7 lb.
- 1.8 L insulated copper heat-exchanger boiler with boiler PID.
- 58 mm chrome-plated brass E61 group, manual lever, hidden shot timer and dual pressure gauges.
- Rotary pump with reservoir or direct-water operation on the R version.
- Articulating no-burn two-hole steam wand and separate hot-water tap.
- The timer records duration but does not stop a shot automatically.
- No volumetric dosing, brew-by-weight, app, integrated grinder, automatic milk or accepted flow-profile claim.
- PID control of the steam boiler does not prove that every use case requires no cooling flush.

- Pro Coffee Gear's page combines conflicting warranty statements and was not used for warranty conclusions.

- Kaffeemacher tested the Mozzafiato Cronometro R sister platform, not this exact Giotto body. Its measurements are not presented as exact Giotto results.
