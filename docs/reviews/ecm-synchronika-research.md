# ECM Synchronika II research ledger

- Canonical route: `/espresso-machine/ecm-synchronika/`

## Official evidence

The route now covers Synchronika II, not the original Synchronika. ECM's current page and instructions list a 0.75 L stainless brew boiler, 2 L stainless steam boiler, PID control, rotary pump, 2.8 L reservoir or direct-water connection, OLED, passive and active preinfusion, dual gauges, and Core Heat electrical group cartridges working with the thermosyphon. ECM states roughly 6 to 6.5 minutes to readiness. Published dimensions are 335 × 490 × 410 mm and weight is 30 kg.

## Independent evidence

Clive Coffee's $3,599 listing and Whole Latte Love's product material and September 20, 2024 video provide current retail, steam-pressure, control, warmup, group, water-mode, and workflow context. Their demonstrations are commercial independent evidence, not Coffeedant testing. ECM's optional Flow Profile Valve is documented separately, so the article requires package confirmation rather than assuming it is included.

## Community evidence

The two cited r/espresso discussions compare Synchronika II with Linea Micra and evaluate the then-new Core Heat design, price, size, steam, preinfusion, and flow-control options. They are useful for identifying buyer priorities. The generation is relatively new, participants self-select, and older original-Synchronika faults cannot be counted as Synchronika II incidence.

## Unknown or unresolved

Long-term generation-specific reliability, Core Heat element life, OLED life, measured readiness across voltages, group temperature stability, steam rate, active-preinfusion flow, pump sound, repair cost, and resale remain unknown. Plumbing results depend on installation and water. Coffeedant has no physical temperature, pressure, sound, taste, teardown, or durability data.

## Editorial decisions

The review changes the old route identity to Synchronika II and removes original-generation warmup assumptions. Espresso, milk, workflow, build, and features score highly because the system combines direct dual-boiler control, fast group heating, rotary pumping, and flexible water. Value reflects the matching grinder, water, installation, and service budget. Bianca and Linea Micra remain the closest different interpretations.
