# Profitec Pro 600 final Quick Steam revision research ledger

Research checked September 24, 2026. The published review is research-led; Coffeedant did not test, measure, photograph, service or disassemble this machine.

## Identity, lifecycle and official evidence

- Scored identity: final North American Pro 600 Quick Steam, called Quick Steam Plus by Whole Latte Love, with spring-loaded joystick valves, front PID and later Fast Heat Up behavior.
- Excluded identity: earlier rotary-valve units and undocumented regional electrical packages.
- Optional configurations: flow-control kit, wood accents and colored magnetic side panels. They are not base features.
- Both checked US specialist pages now treat Pro 600 as retired or discontinued. Clive names Profitec RIDE as the successor.
- The current Profitec product index no longer lists Pro 600 as a machine, though its accessory section still mentions Pro 600 compatibility.

## Specification and workflow record

- Separate 0.75 L stainless brew boiler and 1.0 L stainless steam boiler with independent PID control.
- 58 mm E61 lever group, automatic shot timer, brew-pump and steam-boiler gauges, adjustable expansion valve and switchable steam boiler.
- About 2.8 L removable reservoir, vibration pump and no factory direct plumbing.
- Clive lists about 12 W × 17.63 D × 15.63 H inches without the portafilter, about 22 inches loaded depth, 53 lb and 1,400 W maximum for its North American listing.
- Post-2023 setup can show FIL and require 35 seconds of group flow before heating. Clive says its example boiler setpoints take about 12 minutes, then recommends another 10 minutes for group and portafilter.
- Kaffeemacher measured an earlier unit and reported about 25 minutes plus a flush for target group conditions under its protocol. The different revisions and protocols remain separate.

## Dated availability and price

- Clive displayed $2,399 while explicitly saying the machine was discontinued and no longer carried.
- Whole Latte Love called Quick Steam Plus an old product listing and did not offer normal new-stock purchase.
- Therefore $2,399 is recorded only as a historical US reference, not as a current market or checkout price.
- Any used, open-box or remaining-stock offer needs unit-specific condition, revision, service, warranty and seller-protection checks.

All price and lifecycle states above were checked September 24, 2026.

## Independent and community evidence

- Kaffeemacher says it independently purchased its earlier unit and published a measured warm-up and repeatability review on November 29, 2022.
- Machina Coffee Roasters published a retailer hands-on review on November 18, 2022.
- Owner discussions repeatedly value independent boiler control, steam capacity and the lack of an urgent upgrade path.
- Recurring cautions include E61 warm-up, vibration-pump sound, reservoir filling, manual stopping, optional flow control and revision confusion.

No owner thread supplies the denominator or controlled protocol needed for a failure rate. No earlier thermal result is transferred to the final Fast Heat Up revision without a boundary.

## Claims deliberately not made

Unknown evidence includes final-revision failure incidence, expected lifespan, current serial-specific parts stock, measured noise, measured steam recovery and the condition of any unit Coffeedant has not inspected.

- No Coffeedant access, tasting, pressure, temperature, noise, steam or heat-up measurement.
- No reliability rate, lifespan or defect incidence.
- No claim that a historical $2,399 listing represents current new-stock availability.
- No transfer of older rotary-valve or pre-Fast-Heat-Up behavior to the final revision.
- No claim that optional flow control is standard.
- No claim that boiler-ready means the E61 group and puck path are fully ready.
