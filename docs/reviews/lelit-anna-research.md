# Lelit Anna PL41TEM research ledger

## Scope and method

This ledger supports the Coffeedant route `/espresso-machine/lelit-anna/` and permanent product ID `CD-EM-000200`. The review is research-led. Coffeedant did not operate or bench-test a unit. Current first-party EU records control identity and specifications; independent hands-on work, owner discussion and commercial listings are labelled by role.

Checked September 24, 2026. The editorial update date and sitemap date are both September 24, 2026.

## Exact identity

The reviewed machine is the current European `PL41TEM` Anna in polished stainless steel. The current official page maps it to 220-240 V, 50 Hz and a type E+F plug. It is a PID single-boiler manual espresso machine with a LELIT57 group.

The current official page also lists Australia `PL41TEMDAUS4I80`, Israel `PL41TEM-AS` and UK `PL41TEMD-2G80`. The current US store uses `PESAN06` and 120 V. Older `PL41EM`, `PL41LEM`, `PL41TEM/110` and other Anna records are not interchangeable. They can differ in controls, wand, power, package, branding, warranty and electrical supply.

## Current official facts

- PID boiler temperature controller.
- LELIT57 group compatible with LELIT57 tools.
- Three-way solenoid valve.
- Manometer for coffee pressure, with a green target area on the dial.
- 250 ml brass coffee boiler.
- 2.7 L water tank with a side level window.
- Multidirectional steam and hot-water wand with anti-burn handle.
- Polished stainless body and stainless drip-tray grate.
- Passive cup warmer and lever switches.
- Dimensions: 23 × 38 × 34 cm, L × D × H.
- Mass: 7.5 kg.
- Current EU article: `PL41TEM`, 220-240 V, 50 Hz, type E+F plug.

The current page lists a two-spout LELIT57 holder, 7-9 g one-dose filter, 14-18 g two-dose filter, pod filter, tamper and spoon. The 2024 official sheet also lists a LELIT57 blind filter. Because the live page omits it, the article tells buyers to verify the delivered package.

## Manual boundary

The accessible 48-page exact-model manual is a 2017 document mirrored by Green Plantation. It covers PL41TEM operation, first fill, an 80-130°C PID range, steam-to-coffee boiler refill, weekly care, backflushing and descaling. It is useful for stable workflow and safety concepts but does not define the current package, branding, regional power or warranty. The review explicitly tells owners to follow the current regional manual supplied with their machine.

## Price and availability

Coffee Friend listed Anna PL41TEM at €549 and in stock on September 24, 2026, with delivery quoted at one to five working days. The current official US PESAN06 page listed $599.95. The review uses €549 as a dated EU retailer price, not a universal MSRP. Taxes, voltage, plug, warranty, stock and dealer preparation remain regional.

## Independent evaluation

Coffee Blog published a hands-on review on June 5, 2026. The author states the editorial is not sponsored, but the page contains affiliate links and demonstrates coffee from the author’s business. Reported measurements include 170 ml of milk reaching 60°C in about 36-40 seconds at a 140°C steam setting, about one minute at 125°C, roughly 60-95 seconds to reach steam readiness, and 200 ml of hot water in about 22 seconds. These are one reviewer’s setup and are not presented as Coffeedant measurements or universal outcomes.

Coffee Blog found the machine stable on the counter, the PID display legible and the steam manageable. The review also noted a narrow LELIT57 accessory market, limited tank visibility, no external tank handle, a protruding steam knob, some sharp edges and a manual two-switch brew and water workflow.

Caseta Coffee published a retailer hands-on review on June 1, 2026 after several weeks with a Fiamma Supra 68 grinder, a house blend, an Ethiopian coffee, whole milk and oat milk. Caseta reports 17.5 g test doses, 92°C for its blend, 93-94°C for the Ethiopian coffee and approximately 30-45 seconds of temperature recovery between several espressos. It liked the cup results and steam but noted limited scale clearance, slight grate unevenness, the included tamper and LELIT57 accessory limits. Caseta sells the machine and its listed 1200 W and 26.5 cm depth conflict with current official EU records, so those claims are not transferred.

## Owner and community context

Two exact-model Reddit discussions describe the Anna as capable for short home sessions. Owners say fitted LELIT57 tampers and bottomless holders exist, but the selection is narrower than 58 mm. Multiple comments stress refilling the boiler after steaming and allowing recovery for back-to-back drinks. A few owners report multiple years without major issues, but those self-selected anecdotes cannot establish a reliability rate.

Community evidence is summarised inside the Coffeedant page with no outbound links in that panel. Original thread addresses and evidence limitations remain in the source list.

## Editorial conclusion

The Anna’s case is unusually direct: a current €549 EU price buys PID boiler control, a pressure gauge and a three-way valve in a compact specialist machine. The trade-offs are equally clear. LELIT57 limits accessory choice, the 250 ml single boiler makes milk service sequential, and a separate grinder and scale are required. The review recommends it only when those limits fit the buyer’s drink volume and ownership plan.
