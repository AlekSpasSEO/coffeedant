## Identity and official evidence

This review is for the fifth-generation North American E8 Midnight Silver NAD, article 15722. It is not the earlier EC generation still common in search results and owner videos. The current JURA page and NAD manual control the article number, 27 Hot, Light and Sweet specialties, P.A.G.2 grinder, 5 to 16 g brewing unit, 3.5-inch display, automatic HP3 milk system, optional Wi-Fi Connect V2, Coffee Timer support, cleaning instructions and regional configuration.

Abt, an authorized US retailer, listed article 15722 for $2,599 but unavailable to order on September 23, 2026. That is dated market context, not a promise of current stock or a worldwide price. Tax, finish, warranty and availability can change.

## Independent, community and unknown evidence

WIRED's May 8, 2026 blind comparison reported under-extracted espresso and overheated milk for the E8 in that specific test. The article is valuable independent evidence, but its result is attributed to its protocol and is not generalized to every machine, recipe or owner. A September 4, 2025 independent SD-generation video supplies current-family workflow context while regional differences remain explicit.

Community E8 searches and recipe discussions are used to surface questions about strength, volume, grinder settling, milk cleaning, filters and service. These reports are self-selected, often mix generations and do not support quantified reliability. Unknown evidence includes Coffeedant-measured extraction yield, temperature, foam texture, sound level, energy use, defect rate and expected service life. None is invented.
