# Quick Mill Andreja Premium Evo 0980P-A research ledger

Research checked September 24, 2026. The published review is research-led; Coffeedant did not test, taste with, measure, photograph, service or disassemble this machine.

## Identity and lifecycle

- Scored identity: Quick Mill Andreja Premium Evo, North American product ID 0980P-A, 110 V.
- Official manufacturer evidence establishes the Quick Mill brand and current range context; current US specialist records establish the scored configuration because Quick Mill's home page does not publish this SKU.
- The checked US model is a reservoir-fed E61 heat exchanger with a Sirai mechanical pressurestat. It is not a PID dual boiler.
- Current Chris Coffee and 1st in Coffee records govern the scored identity. Both showed 0980P-A in stock on September 24, 2026.
- Flow control, joystick valves and fixed-water plumbing are not documented as included with the checked base SKU. Dealer-installed and owner-installed equipment remains a separate configuration.
- Excluded identities: earlier Andreja and Andreja Premium machines, regional 230 V 0980 versions, retailer records that describe PID or shot-timer controls, and modified machines.
- Andreja was absent from Quick Mill's current home-page lineup on September 24, 2026. Coffeedant did not find a formal discontinuation notice, while current US specialists still listed new stock.

## Specification and safety record

- The current US importer record describes one 1.8 L T.E.A.-coated heat-exchanger boiler serving brewing, steam and hot water.
- The 58 mm E61 thermosiphon group uses a manual lever and three-way brew circuit.
- A Sirai mechanical pressurestat regulates boiler pressure. Dual analog gauges display pump and boiler pressure; they do not measure puck temperature or beverage yield.
- Water comes from a 3 L bottom-fed reservoir. Chris Coffee says the former direct-connect kit has not been available since 2020, so the current base machine is treated as reservoir-only.
- Pump record: 52 W Ulka vibration pump with Quick Mill's Pulsar dampener.
- North American electrical record: 110 V, 1,400 W and 15 A. The rating plate must be checked on every unit.
- Current specialist dimensions are about 11.5 inches wide, 17.5 inches deep and 16 inches high with the cup rail. Weight is about 46 lb.
- Initial boiler fill must follow the manual before heating. Hot E61 metal, steam, hot water, mains electricity and residual group pressure require normal espresso-machine precautions. Electrical, boiler, pressure-control and heater work belongs with a competent technician.

## Dated price and package

- Chris Coffee and 1st in Coffee each displayed $1,995 and in-stock status on September 24, 2026.
- Chris Coffee and 1st in Coffee each described a two-year seller warranty. Warranty administration, coverage, freight and fulfillment remain seller-specific.
- Specialist records identify portafilters, baskets, blind basket, tamper and cleaning accessories, but a delivered or used unit must be checked item by item.
- A used price needs adjustments for generation, voltage, water history, scale, pressurestat and heating condition, pump health, E61 service, accessories, modifications, freight, tax and available technical support.

## Independent, owner and community evidence

- iDrinkCoffeeCanada published the exact-line video `iDrinkCoffee.com Review - Quick Mill Andreja Premium Redesigned Espresso Machine` on October 18, 2018. It shows a redesigned Andreja, espresso and latte workflow, not a controlled current-SKU performance test.
- The Home-Barista Andreja buyer guide provides independent comparative context, while its generation and test-era limits are preserved.
- Home-Barista owner and technician discussion connects ready-light cycling to pressurestat operation without establishing one universal setting, flush or beverage temperature.
- Reddit upgrade discussion repeatedly raises milk-drink workflow, grinder priority and plumbing assumptions.
- One used-value discussion illustrates why age, condition and service history matter. Another follows a single broken second-hand machine and diagnostic ideas. Neither establishes a current fair price or a model-wide failure rate.
- Direct-plumb examples require separate inspection because the retired kit and owner modifications do not define the current base machine.

No owner thread supplies a controlled denominator for failure incidence or expected service life. Voltage, generation, water, grinder, roast, warm-up, service history and modifications differ.

This community evidence supplies bounded fit, workflow and inspection questions only. Unknown evidence includes failure incidence, remaining service life, measured warm-up, measured puck temperature, measured flow, measured pressure, measured noise, measured steam recovery and the condition of any unit Coffeedant has not inspected; those points remain unknown.

## Claims deliberately not made

- No Coffeedant access, tasting, temperature, pressure, flow, noise, steam, warm-up or extraction-yield measurement.
- No claim that a boiler-pressure gauge or pressurestat state equals puck temperature.
- No universal warm-up time, cooling-flush routine, steaming time, reliability rate or lifespan.
- No claim that the North American 0980P-A includes PID, a shot timer, a rotary pump, flow control or factory plumbing.

- No claim that optional joystick valves, flow-control kits or owner plumbing are factory equipment.
