# Espresso machines with a grinder: research ledger

- Route: `/espresso-machine/built-in-grinder/`
- Research completed: September 25, 2026
- Method: current manufacturer pages and manuals control model identity and operation; independent evaluations supply observed workflow; owner discussions supply questions, not failure rates.
- Scope: current US Breville Barista Pro BES878, current US Breville Barista Express Impress BES876, US De’Longhi Magnifica Evo ECAM29084SB with LatteCrema, and fifth-generation North American JURA E8 Midnight Silver article 15722.

## Decision frame

The query includes distinct product types. Barista Pro and Express Impress grind into a removable portafilter, so puck preparation, extraction judgment and manual milk remain with the user. Magnifica Evo and E8 contain an internal brew unit, so they grind, form, brew and eject the puck inside the appliance. The guide keeps these categories separate and recommends by task ownership rather than treating every integrated grinder as bean-to-cup.

## Exact identities and dated price context

- Breville Barista Pro BES878: current US product page and instruction book. Breville displayed $849.95. Current documentation specifies 30 grind settings and Baratza European precision burrs. Older grinder descriptions are generation-limited.
- Breville Barista Express Impress BES876: current US product page and instruction book. Breville displayed $799.95. Exact identity includes 25 settings, dose correction and lever-assisted tamping. It is not a one-touch milk machine.
- De’Longhi Magnifica Evo ECAM29084SB: exact US product page and instructions. De’Longhi displayed $899.95. This is the seven-recipe LatteCrema panel, not a manual-frother variant or Magnifica Evo Next.
- JURA E8 Midnight Silver NAD article 15722: official fifth-generation North American page and NAD manual. Abt displayed $2,599 but unavailable status. The guide treats this as a dated reference, not guaranteed stock.

## Independent and community evidence

- Barista Pro: Coffeeness and Lifestyle Lab for grinder, interface, espresso and steam workflow. Older unit context is disclosed.
- Express Impress: Serious Eats and WIRED for assisted preparation, consistency and control trade-offs.
- Magnifica Evo: Whole Latte Love and Reviewed for family distinctions and observed automatic drink workflow.
- E8: WIRED May 2026 blind protocol plus an independent current-generation video in the dedicated review. One protocol is not generalized to every unit.
- Owner context: recent Breville integration discussion, one-year Barista Pro notes, Express Impress buyer discussion, Magnifica Evo owner thread and JURA E8 search pool. Self-selection, mixed generations, water and care differences prevent reliability-rate claims.
