- Scope: current US Breville Bambino Plus BES500, current North American Gaggia Classic Pro E24, current US Breville Barista Express BES870XL, and current US De’Longhi Magnifica Evo ECAM29084SB.

## Decision boundary

The guide represents four different first-owner workflows rather than ranking near-identical machines: compact semi-automatic with a separate grinder, traditional 58 mm single boiler, integrated-grinder semi-automatic, and bean-to-cup automatic. It excludes capsule systems, discontinued normal-stock recommendations, marketplace-only brands without a clear support route, and premium prosumer machines outside first-station intent.

## Limitations

- No Coffeedant side-by-side test, taste test, temperature measurement, sound measurement, recovery test or representative reliability dataset is claimed.
- Complete system cost varies with grinder, water, scale, care supplies, tax and promotions.
- Gaggia E24 evidence is separated from the coated-boiler 2023 Evo generation.
- De’Longhi Magnifica Evo evidence is restricted to US ECAM29084SB with LatteCrema.
- Barista Express evidence is restricted to current US BES870XL; older BES860 and regional Sage packages are not treated as identical.
- Bambino Plus evidence is restricted to US BES500; standard Bambino BES450 is a separate machine.
