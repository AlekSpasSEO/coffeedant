# Prosumer espresso machines under $1,000 research ledger

Checked September 25, 2026. The guide uses a strict machine-only ceiling before tax and accessories. Model identity and specified operation come from manufacturer records; independent sources add bounded workflow observations; community sources identify questions, not failure rates.

## Included exact models

- Gaggia Classic Pro E24: current North American 110–120 V brass-boiler model, $549 direct. It is not the 2023 coated-boiler Classic Evo Pro.
- Lelit Anna: current US PESAN06 at $599.95 and EU PL41TEM at €549 are related regional articles. Voltage, plug, package and dealer terms remain regional.
- Rancilio Silvia V6: current core North American 120 V thermostat-controlled single boiler, checked at $995. It is not Silvia Pro X.
- Flair 58 Plus 2: current US package at $699 with integrated three-level group controller. It is not the original Flair 58 or earlier Plus.

## Exclusions and boundaries

Profitec GO, Lelit Victoria and Gaggia Classic GT were above the durable $1,000 ceiling at the checked prices. Integrated-grinder semi-automatics and superautomatics answer different workflow questions. Used and temporary sale listings were not allowed to move an above-cap model into the shortlist.

## Evidence discipline

All four machines require a separate grinder for fresh-bean espresso. Gaggia, Lelit and Rancilio are sequential single boilers. Flair has no pump, boiler or milk system. The page makes no Coffeedant temperature, pressure, recovery, sound, extraction, reliability or service-life measurements. Owner material is self-selected and cannot establish population-wide outcomes.
