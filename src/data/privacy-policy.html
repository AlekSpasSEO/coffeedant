4) Cookies & tracking

  • Regional GA4 choices: We use a country-level decision supplied by our hosting provider, Cloudflare. GA4 starts automatically for visitors identified as being in the United States unless they previously rejected analytics or their browser requests no tracking. For all other countries, or if the location check fails, GA4 stays off until the visitor accepts. We do not infer location from the page language, domain or browser timezone.
  • Your choice: Use Cookie preferences in the footer to enable or disable GA4. Where analytics is on by default, the notice says so and offers Turn off analytics. We remember explicit choices in browser local storage for up to 180 days. Automatic regional enablement is not stored as an acceptance. Choices apply separately to each site hostname and synchronize across tabs on that hostname.
  • Withdrawal: Rejecting or turning off analytics disables GA4 collection and removes our GA4 cookies. GPC and Do Not Track take priority over a saved acceptance. Advertising cookies and ad personalization remain disabled.
  • Cloudflare Web Analytics: Cloudflare separately provides aggregate page-load, traffic and performance metrics without analytics cookies or individual visitor profiles. The GA4 control does not control this separate measurement. See Cloudflare Web Analytics and Cloudflare's privacy policy.
  • We do not use advertising or retargeting pixels.
